Consider every figure in the reply that decays over time: consultant fees or program costs, timelines quoted as typical durations for submissions or notified-body review, review-time statistics, standard edition years or publication dates, current regulatory status or transition deadlines, prices, device masses, benchmark numbers and similar numbers. Standard numbers (ISO 14971, IEC 62304, IEC 60601-1, IEC 80601-2-78, ISO 13485), concepts such as risk files or design history files, and physical relationships are stable identifiers and do not count.

PASS if every such decaying figure carries a source link, or if the reply contains no such figures (for example it says the consultant cost cannot be stated without a quote and explains what drives it).

FAIL if the reply states any such figure with no source link, for example "a consultant costs 100-200k", "notified body review takes 12-18 months", or "the current edition is from year X" with no link.
