I'm building visual-inertial odometry for a small ground robot on a Jetson Orin Nano, with a USB UVC camera (1280x720, 30 fps, rolling shutter) and a separate ICM-42688 IMU at 400 Hz read over SPI, running OpenVINS on ROS 2 Jazzy. I ran the Kalibr camera-IMU calibration and put its T_cam_imu and the noise values into the config. The estimate is fine when the robot creeps along, but the position blows up within a few seconds of any sharp turn or bump. The camera and the IMU are separate devices on separate buses, each read by its own simple ROS 2 driver node. I'm planning to retune the IMU noise parameters and raise the number of tracked features per frame. What should I change?
