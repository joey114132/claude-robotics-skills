We're building a tracked sprayer robot for a pear orchard, about 3 m row spacing, and I want to keep the sensor suite cheap. Plan is a dual-band u-blox ZED-F9P on the roof with NTRIP corrections over LTE, record the row waypoints once with the robot, and have it follow them with a pure-pursuit controller at about 1 m/s, no LiDAR or cameras. In an open test field this gave us roughly 2 cm tracking error. What should I configure for the RTK setup to get the same accuracy when we move to the orchard, and anything else I should do before we spray?
