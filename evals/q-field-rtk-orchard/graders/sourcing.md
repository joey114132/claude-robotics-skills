Decaying specifics are prices, masses, payloads, release dates, version-support windows, benchmark or accuracy figures taken from a named product or paper, and claims about what a software version added. Stable identifiers (package names, receiver model names, standard numbers, textbook concepts, physical relationships), the user's own figures (2 cm, 1 m/s, 3 m), and generic engineering numbers or typical ranges the reply states as rules of thumb or derives itself (for example 'RTK fixed is centimetre-level in open sky', a stop-after-N-seconds policy) do not count. Only a figure attributed to a named product datasheet or a specific paper counts.

PASS if every decaying specific in the reply either carries a source link or is absent. A reply with no such specifics passes.

FAIL if the reply states such a figure with no source link, for example a sensor price, a vendor's quoted accuracy, a paper's error figure, or a software release claim.
