expected_outcome: |
  An expert answer must catch that the plan cannot work as framed. GPS and other radio signals do not propagate through seawater, so as soon as the AUV dives at the planned 15 m depth the u-blox fix is gone, navsat_transform_node and the Nav2 GPS waypoint follower get no input, and the map-to-odom correction never arrives. Underwater, position must come from dead reckoning (DVL for bottom-relative velocity plus an IMU/AHRS or INS, plus pressure depth), which drifts without bound, bounded by acoustic positioning (USBL or LBL transponders) or by periodically surfacing for a GPS fix. The recommendation must change accordingly: build the estimator around DVL+IMU+depth (for example an EKF in robot_localization fed by DVL velocity), use GPS only at the surface for launch/recovery and drift correction, add USBL or LBL if absolute position during the dive matters, and define behaviour when DVL bottom lock is lost. It should also note that standard Nav2 assumes a ground/planar robot and is not a ready fit for a 3D vehicle with a velocity-over-ground sensor, or that waypoints should be tracked in a local frame. Primary source: Blue Robotics DVL integration guide states DVLs "use a dead reckoning algorithm ... They do not use an external source like GPS to determine global position" and that "To obtain accurate global positioning ... a global positioning system such as GPS or USBL is necessary": https://bluerobotics.com/learn/dvl-a50-integration/ . Corroboration (Wikipedia, Autonomous underwater vehicle): "Radio waves cannot penetrate water very far, so as soon as an AUV dives it loses its GPS signal"; AUVs use dead reckoning, inertial navigation, DVL, LBL/USBL acoustic positioning, or surface for a GPS fix: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_underwater_vehicle

We're building a 1.4 m torpedo-style AUV for a 600 m seabed transect survey, cruising at about 15 m depth. The plan is to reuse the ROS 2 Jazzy GPS waypoint stack: a u-blox ZED-F9P with the antenna on top of the hull feeding robot_localization's navsat_transform_node, an EKF fusing that with the IMU, and the Nav2 waypoint follower driving the thrusters through a small cmd_vel-to-fin/prop bridge. Can you review this architecture and tell us what to tune first so the vehicle hits its 12 waypoints within about 2 m?
