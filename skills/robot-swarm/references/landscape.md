# Swarm & Distributed Robotics Landscape — verified snapshot
**Verified: 2026-08-05.** Entries were confirmed against live sources on this date — re-verify anything before relying on it if this snapshot is more than ~6 months old.

## Simulators & experiment tooling
- **ARGoS3** — modular multi-engine physics simulator built for large-scale swarms; several physics engines can run in one experiment, benchmarked at physics-accurate simulation of thousands of robots faster than real time. MIT-licensed since v3. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/ilpincy/argos3
- **argos3-kilobot** — ARGoS plugin modeling Kilobots with 2D dynamics, object pushing, and density-aware communication occlusion; wraps `kilolib.h` so unmodified Kilobot controller code runs in simulation. Status: research (no license stated in repo). Source: https://github.com/ilpincy/argos3-kilobot
- **SIERRA** — command-line research-automation pipeline (input generation, execution, postprocessing, artifact generation) for swarm experiments; supports ARGoS, ROS 1 + Gazebo, and ROS 1 on real hardware, plus SLURM/PBS/AWS Batch execution. MIT. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/jharwell/sierra
- **SwarmLab** — MATLAB drone-swarm simulator implementing Olfati-Saber's flocking algorithm and Vásárhelyi's variant of the Vicsek model, with quadcopter, fixed-wing, and point-mass models. MIT. Status: research (low commit count, community interest). Source: https://github.com/lis-epfl/swarmlab

## Swarm programming & behavior libraries
- **Buzz** — programming language for heterogeneous swarms with a purely distributed runtime; bottom-up primitives (neighbor map/reduce/filter) plus top-down team management, integrating with ARGoS, ROS, and Khepera IV. MIT. Status: research (repo's most recent documented activity is an ICRA 2017 tutorial — verify before adopting). Source: https://github.com/buzz-lang/Buzz ; paper: arXiv:1507.05946. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.05946
- **ROS2swarm** — ROS 2 library of swarm behavioral primitives (aggregation, attraction, dispersion, flocking, random walk, voter model, majority rule) with a hardware protection layer; supports TurtleBot3 Burger/Waffle Pi, Jackal UGV, Thymio II on ROS 2 Dashing/Foxy/Galactic. Apache 2.0. Status: **not maintained** (stated in README) — useful as reference code, not a base. Source: https://github.com/ROS2swarm/ROS2swarm ; paper: arXiv:2405.02438. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02438
- **Self-organizing Nervous System (SoNS)** — architecture where robots autonomously form, maintain, and reconfigure dynamic multi-level hierarchies, giving locally centralized coordination without losing scalability or fault tolerance; demonstrated on a heterogeneous aerial-ground swarm and simulated to 250 robots. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2401.13103. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13103

## Decentralized planning, formation & collision avoidance
- **EGO-Swarm (ego-planner-swarm)** — fully autonomous, decentralized, asynchronous quadrotor swarm planner for cluttered unknown environments using only onboard resources; ROS 1 on master with a `ros2_version` branch. GPLv3, ZJU FAST-Lab. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/ZJU-FAST-Lab/ego-planner-swarm ; paper: arXiv:2011.04183. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.04183
- **Swarm-Formation** — distributed formation flight in dense environments using a differentiable graph-theory-based formation-similarity cost combined with obstacle avoidance and dynamic feasibility (ICRA 2022). GPLv3, ZJU FAST-Lab. Status: maintained ("still working on extending the system"). Source: https://github.com/ZJU-FAST-Lab/Swarm-Formation
- **MADER** — multi-agent trajectory planner for dynamic environments with moving obstacles and other agents (T-RO). BSD-3-Clause, MIT ACL. Status: research (mature, low recent activity). Source: https://github.com/mit-acl/mader
- **Contingency Model-based Control (CMC)** — decentralized cooperative collision avoidance with *no* inter-robot communication: offline consensual rules plus per-robot contingency trajectories and bisecting-plane constraints give recursive feasibility and plug-and-play swarm entry. Status: research (2026). Source: arXiv:2512.20391. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20391

## Decentralized state estimation & collaborative SLAM
- **Swarm-SLAM** — open-source sparse decentralized collaborative SLAM for multi-robot systems; inertial, lidar, stereo, and RGB-D support with inter-robot loop-closure prioritization to cut communication; ROS 2 implementation, validated on three robots over an ad-hoc network. MIT, MISTLab. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/MISTLab/Swarm-SLAM ; paper: arXiv:2301.06230. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.06230
- **Kimera-Multi** — fully distributed peer-to-peer metric-semantic C-SLAM over a lightweight UDP layer with robust rejection of incorrect inter/intra-robot loop closures; released datasets from 8 robots on the MIT campus, examples with 6. Ubuntu 20.04 / ROS Noetic. Status: research (actively updated). Source: https://github.com/MIT-SPARK/Kimera-Multi
- **Omni-swarm** — decentralized omnidirectional visual-inertial-UWB state estimation for aerial swarms, reporting centimeter-level relative accuracy without external infrastructure (T-RO). GPLv3, HKUST Aerial Robotics. Status: research. Source: https://github.com/HKUST-Aerial-Robotics/Omni-swarm
- **D2SLAM** — decentralized *and* distributed collaborative visual-inertial SLAM for aerial swarms, covering near-field relative-pose accuracy and far-field globally consistent trajectories (T-RO 2024). LGPL-3, HKUST Aerial Robotics. Status: research. Source: https://github.com/HKUST-Aerial-Robotics/D2SLAM

## Hardware platforms, positioning & testbeds
- **Crazyflie 2.1 Brushless (Bitcraze)** — 32 g open-source nano-quadrotor, 34–37 g takeoff weight, ~40 g max recommended payload, ~10 min flight, 60 min charge, >1 km line-of-sight with Crazyradio PA; the default cheap indoor swarm unit. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.bitcraze.io/products/crazyflie-2-1-brushless/
- **Crazyswarm2** — ROS 2 stack for Crazyflie 2.1(+), 2.1 Brushless, Bolt-based customs, and Flapper Nimble+; uses broadcast where possible to cut radio count, integrates `motion_capture_tracking`, ships a simulation mode. MIT, community-maintained (limited vendor support). Status: maintained. Source: https://imrclab.github.io/crazyswarm2/
- **Loco Positioning System (Bitcraze)** — UWB (DWM1000) indoor positioning, ~10 cm accuracy; TWR mode supports only one tag at a time, TDoA 2/TDoA 3 let unlimited drones position passively — the mode choice is what makes or breaks a Crazyflie swarm. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.bitcraze.io/documentation/system/positioning/loco-positioning-system/
- **e-puck2 (GCtronic)** — 70 mm, 130 g educational/research swarm robot: 8 IR proximity sensors, time-of-flight to 2 m, VGA camera, 4 digital mics, IMU, USB/Bluetooth 2.0/BLE/WiFi; Webots model available. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.gctronic.com/doc/index.php/e-puck2
- **Robotarium (Georgia Tech)** — remotely accessible multi-robot testbed with provably collision-free execution via barrier certificates, automated recharging and global tracking; free remote submission plus an MIT-licensed Python simulator (single-integrator and unicycle models) for offline development. Status: maintained (facility + simulator). Source: https://github.com/robotarium/robotarium_python_simulator ; paper: arXiv:1609.04730. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.04730
- **Small-scale testbed survey** — comparison of 23 small-scale testbeds for connected vehicles and robot swarms across 62 sense-plan-act characteristics, with a maintained online table; the fastest way to find an existing testbed instead of building one. Status: research (living resource). Source: arXiv:2408.14199 (table: https://bassamlab.github.io/testbeds-survey). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14199

## Communication & scaling infrastructure
- **Zenoh** — pub/sub plus store/query and compute protocol with peer and router (`zenohd`) deployments, widely used as a ROS 2 alternative transport where DDS discovery does not scale. EPL 2.0 / Apache 2.0. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/eclipse-zenoh/zenoh
- **SwarmUS** — open hardware+software add-on board that grants existing robots swarm features: UWB relative localization, WiFi local communication, Buzz-based distributed coordination, ROS emulation; demonstrated on TurtleBot3 Burger and Pioneer 2DX. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2203.02643. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02643
- **SwarmUpdate** — hierarchical software-update synchronization (SwarmSync) plus layer-freezing deep-learning model patching (SwarmModelPatch) for heterogeneous UAV swarms of dozens to hundreds, benchmarked in ARGoS against auction- and gossip-based baselines. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2503.13784. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13784

## China & Asia-Pacific ecosystem
- **High Great 高巨创新** — Shenzhen maker of formation/light-show drones (RIFF, EMO, TAKE, G10, FYLO Mini, HULA education platform) covering outdoor, indoor, and VTOL formation systems; own site reports 100,000+ formation sorties and 30,000+ shows. Chinese media additionally report a 16,000-drone Guinness record at the 2025 Liuyang fireworks festival — treat record claims as secondary-sourced. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.hg-fly.com/ ; record claim: https://www.sohu.com/a/1045400949_122810496
- **Prometheus (阿木实验室 / Amov Lab)** — Chinese open-source autonomous-UAV platform on PX4 + ROS bundling control, planning, and target detection with swarm demos; Apache 2.0 but README restricts use to non-commercial, ~1,700 commits, active community support. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/amov-lab/Prometheus
- **ZJU FAST-Lab (Zhejiang University)** — the reference open-source group for decentralized aerial swarm planning; publishes EGO-Planner/EGO-Swarm and Swarm-Formation (both listed above) under GPLv3. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/orgs/ZJU-FAST-Lab/repositories
- **HKUST Aerial Robotics Group** — publishes Omni-swarm and D2SLAM (above), the main open-source line for infrastructure-free decentralized swarm state estimation with visual-inertial + UWB fusion. Status: research. Source: https://github.com/HKUST-Aerial-Robotics

## Task allocation, collective decision-making & recent research (arXiv)
- **FORMICA** — communication-free multi-robot task allocation: each robot predicts the *distribution* of teammates' bids (mean-field, O(T) instead of O(NT)) and is trained end-to-end to minimize allocation regret; +17% reward at 16 robots/64 tasks, generalizing to 256 robots/4096 tasks. Status: research (2026). Source: arXiv:2602.18622. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18622
- **Auction-Consensus with learned bidding** — replaces CBBA's hand-crafted greedy scoring with an RL-trained neural bidding policy under centralized-training/decentralized-execution, keeping the standard auction and consensus phases; good entry point for what CBBA does and where it is suboptimal. Status: research (2026). Source: arXiv:2605.21932. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21932
- **Bayesian decentralized decision-making** — swarm of simple robots choosing the safer of two areas from Poisson event rates using conjugate priors and confidence-adaptive behavior; sample-efficient collective decision benchmark. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2511.22225. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22225
- **Asymmetric physics for quadruped swarms** — end-to-end vision-based decentralized navigation trained with a non-differentiable high-fidelity simulator paired with differentiable surrogates; 512 quadrupeds trained together, zero-shot transfer to six physical robots using one depth camera each, no communication. Status: research (2026). Source: arXiv:2606.23153. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23153
- **Proactive-reactive intermittent-fault handling** — self-organized backup network paths plus one-shot likelihood-ratio tests across a multiplex network to detect and route around intermittent (not permanent) faults during formation control. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2509.19246. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19246
- **Swarm Oracle** — Byzantine-fault-tolerant consensus over a multi-stakeholder robot swarm feeding verified physical-world data to blockchain smart contracts, with a token reputation system for recovery after attacks. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2509.15956. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15956
- **Underwater swarm robotics review** — synthesis of bio-inspired coordination, acoustic/optical/hybrid communication constraints, and system design for marine swarms, with a classification across communication dependency, adaptability, energy, and scalability. Status: research (2026). Source: arXiv:2601.12244. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12244
