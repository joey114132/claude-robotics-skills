# Robot Simulation & Digital Twin Landscape — verified snapshot
**Verified: 2026-08-05.** Entries were confirmed against live sources on this date — re-verify anything before relying on it if this snapshot is more than ~6 months old.

## General-purpose robot simulators
- **Gazebo** — the ROS-ecosystem default simulator; named releases pair with ROS 2 distros. Current LTS line: Jetty (Sep 2025, EOL May 2031) and Harmonic (Sep 2023, EOL May 2029); Ionic reaches EOL Dec 2026; a rolling stream ships as Rotary. Status: maintained. Source: https://gazebosim.org/docs/latest/releases/
- **MuJoCo / MJX** — physics engine favored for contact-rich dynamics and control research; MJX is the JAX-based GPU frontend documented alongside it. Status: maintained. Source: https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/mjx.html
- **Webots** — open-source desktop robot simulator built on a Qt GUI, an ODE fork for physics, and an OpenGL 3.3 renderer (wren); robots programmable in C/C++/Python/Java/MATLAB/ROS, scenes exportable to interactive HTML. Status: maintained. Source: https://cyberbotics.com/
- **CoppeliaSim** — V-REP successor with distributed per-object scripting; bundles five physics engines (MuJoCo, Bullet, ODE, Newton, Vortex Dynamics). Latest release V4.10.0 rev0; Lite/Pro/Edu licensing with Edu free for education only. Status: commercial (free educational tier). Source: https://www.coppeliarobotics.com/
- **Drake** — C++/Python toolbox for robot dynamics analysis and optimization-based control design, originated at MIT's Robot Locomotion Group and now led by Toyota Research Institute; simulates friction, contact, and aerodynamics with emphasis on exposing mathematical structure. Status: maintained. Source: https://drake.mit.edu/
- **CARLA** — open-source autonomous-driving simulator with open urban assets and configurable sensors/weather; the `ue5-dev` branch targets Unreal Engine 5.5 and requires Ubuntu 22.04/24.04 or Windows 11, RTX 3070+, 32 GB+ RAM. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/carla-simulator/carla

## GPU-parallel simulation for learning
- **Newton** — GPU-accelerated physics engine built on NVIDIA Warp and OpenUSD, a Linux Foundation project initiated by Disney Research, Google DeepMind and NVIDIA; MuJoCo Warp is its primary rigid-body backend, Apache-2.0 code, requires an NVIDIA GPU (Maxwell+, driver 545+) with CPU-only fallback on macOS. Newton 1.0 GA announced March 2026, shipping MuJoCo Warp and Kamino solvers plus Vertex Block Descent for deformables, SDF collision, and hydroelastic contact. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/newton-physics/newton ; https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/newton-adds-contact-rich-manipulation-and-locomotion-capabilities-for-industrial-robotics/
- **MuJoCo Warp (MJWarp)** — GPU-optimized MuJoCo for NVIDIA hardware, maintained jointly by Google DeepMind and NVIDIA as part of the Newton project; supports rigid bodies, contacts, soft bodies, cloth and SDFs, but not the IMPLICITFAST integrator, PGS solver, plugin actuators, or Warp differentiability. Reported up to 252x MJX speed on locomotion and 475x on manipulation on RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_warp ; https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/newton-adds-contact-rich-manipulation-and-locomotion-capabilities-for-industrial-robotics/
- **Isaac Lab** — NVIDIA's GPU-accelerated open-source framework unifying RL, imitation learning and motion planning on Isaac Sim. Current line: Isaac Lab 3.0.0 Beta 2 on Isaac Sim 6.0.0/6.0.1; BSD-3-Clause core with Apache-2.0 `isaaclab_mimic`, but full-feature workflows depend on proprietary-licensed Isaac Sim and cuRobo. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab
- **MuJoCo Playground** — Google DeepMind's GPU-accelerated environment suite for robot learning and sim-to-real, runnable on MJX (JAX) or MuJoCo Warp; covers dm_control classics, quadruped/biped locomotion, dexterous and non-prehensile manipulation, and vision tasks via the MJWarp batch renderer. Apache-2.0. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_playground
- **mjlab** — lightweight robot-learning framework pairing Isaac Lab's manager-based composition API with MuJoCo Warp physics, single-command install, minimal dependencies, direct access to native MuJoCo structures; ships velocity-tracking, motion-imitation and manipulation references. Status: research (open-source). Source: arXiv:2601.22074. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22074
- **Genesis** — multi-physics simulation platform for physical AI combining a unified engine (rigid, FEM, MPM, PBD/SPH, cloth, fluids, IPC contact with a multi-solver coupler), the Nyx renderer and the Quadrants cross-platform compiler (CUDA, ROCm, Metal, Vulkan, x86, ARM64). Version 1.0 released May 2026; started as an academic project Dec 2024, now backed by Genesis AI. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/Genesis-Embodied-AI/Genesis
- **ManiSkill3** — GPU-parallel manipulation simulation and training framework on the SAPIEN engine, claiming 30,000+ FPS RGBD+segmentation collection on an RTX 4090; RSS 2025. Rigid-body environments Apache-2.0; assets CC BY-NC 4.0. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/haosulab/ManiSkill
- **Kamino** — GPU constrained-optimization solver in NVIDIA Warp, integrated into Newton, that natively simulates kinematic loops and heterogeneous worlds without tree approximations or mimic joints; solves contact as an NCP rather than a convexified relaxation. Demonstrated RL training on a biped with six nested kinematic loops across 4096 parallel environments on one GPU. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2603.16536. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16536

## Contact & physics fidelity — benchmarks and comparative studies
- **SimBenchmark** — ETH RSL's contact-simulation accuracy/speed benchmark across RaiSim, Bullet, ODE, MuJoCo and DART over seven tests (rolling/friction, bouncing, 666-ball hard and elastic contact, ANYmal PD/momentum/energy). Findings include Bullet's position-level drift without post-solver error correction and ODE/DART LCP failures to reproduce slip in some conditions. Status: research (reference benchmark). Source: https://leggedrobotics.github.io/SimBenchmark/
- **Contact Models in Robotics: a Comparative Analysis** — survey of the contact/friction models and numerical methods behind current simulators (Signorini, Coulomb, maximum dissipation), exposing each engine's physical relaxations, with quantitative benchmark criteria and open-source C++ implementations; shows some common approximations severely widen the reality gap. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2304.06372. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06372
- **Validating Robotics Simulators on Real-World Impacts** — identifies system-specific contact parameters for Drake, MuJoCo and Bullet against real impact data; simulators reproduce inelastic impacts well and fail on elastic impacts, and on a Cassie biped landing capture bulk motion but are limited by model mismatch. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2110.00541. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.00541
- **A Survey of Robotic Navigation and Manipulation with Physics Simulators in the Era of Embodied AI** — compares simulator properties, navigation/manipulation feature coverage and hardware requirements, with a companion resource of benchmark datasets, metrics and platforms; useful for shortlisting a simulator under a fixed hardware budget. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2505.01458. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01458

## Sim-to-real: system identification, actuator modeling, randomization
- **The Reality Gap in Robotics: Challenges, Solutions, and Best Practices** — the current reference survey of the sim-to-real landscape: causes of the gap, solution families (domain randomization, real-to-sim, state/action abstraction, sim-real co-training) and evaluation metrics. Status: research (survey). Source: arXiv:2510.20808. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20808
- **SPI-Active** — two-stage sampling-based physical-parameter identification for legged robots with an active-exploration stage that maximizes Fisher information of collected real trajectories; reports 42–63% improvement over baselines on locomotion sim-to-real, without requiring differentiable dynamics or direct torque measurement. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2505.14266. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14266
- **Actuator Reality Shaping** — inverts the usual approach: instead of raising simulator fidelity, a two-degree-of-freedom feedforward/feedback controller per joint shapes real actuator closed-loop behavior to match the idealized second-order reference dynamics used in sim, enabling zero-shot deployment without learned actuator models. Validated on a high-gear-ratio servo, a 7-DOF arm, a wheeled-legged robot and a humanoid. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2607.02205. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02205
- **How Should a Simulation-to-Reality Transfer Budget Be Spent?** — controlled study of allocating real-robot measurement time between system identification and domain randomization; finds a small number of identification rollouts closes most of the gap, and once any real data exists, training at estimated parameters beats training over a widened randomization band. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2606.22062. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22062

## Measuring the sim-to-real gap: policy-evaluation benchmarks
- **SIMPLER / SimplerEnv** — real-to-sim evaluation framework for manipulation policies using visual matching (real imagery overlaid on sim) and variant aggregation; scores sim-real alignment with Mean Maximum Rank Violation (MMRV) and Pearson correlation rather than absolute success. CoRL 2024, MIT license, runs on ManiSkill2 (CPU) or ManiSkill3 (GPU). Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/simpler-env/SimplerEnv
- **REALM** — real-to-sim validated benchmark for VLA generalization, explicitly built to correlate simulated and real performance; 15 perturbation factors, 7 manipulation skills, 3,500+ objects, evaluated on π₀, π₀-FAST and GR00T N1.5. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2512.19562. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19562
- **Robot Policy Evaluation for Sim-to-Real Transfer: A Benchmarking Perspective** — position paper on benchmark design for generalist manipulation policies: high visual-fidelity simulation, systematically increasing task complexity and perturbation, and quantifying alignment between real and simulated performance. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2508.11117. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11117

## Digital twin construction & synchronization
- **SyncTwin** — digital-twin framework combining fast object-level 3D reconstruction (VGGT) with continuous real-to-sim synchronization via point-cloud segmentation and colored-ICP registration, closing a real-to-sim-to-real loop so planners compute collision-free trajectories against a live twin under occlusion. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2601.09920. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09920
- **GaussTwin** — real-time twin unifying position-based dynamics and discrete Cosserat rods for physically grounded simulation with Gaussian splatting for rendering and photometric visual correction; Gaussians anchored to physical primitives with SE(3) updates, validated on a Franka Research 3. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2603.05108. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05108
- **ArtiTwinSplat** — builds articulated photo-realistic digital twins of objects directly from RGB-D video with unsupervised articulation discovery, no CAD models or manual annotation, producing twins usable by downstream planning and learning. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2606.24628. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24628

## Scenario & test-suite design
- **ASAM OpenSCENARIO DSL** — standardized domain-specific language for describing test scenarios for large-scale verification and validation of automated driving, usable in virtual environments, proving grounds and HIL; version 2.2.0 released 19 March 2026, currently in maintenance mode. Emphasizes composability and scenario-space exploration versus the trigger-action style of OpenSCENARIO XML. Status: maintained standard. Source: https://www.asam.net/standards/detail/openscenario-dsl/
- **CARLA ScenarioRunner** — traffic-scenario definition and execution engine for CARLA, supporting a Python interface, OpenSCENARIO and OpenSCENARIO 2.0; release 0.9.16 pairs with CARLA 0.9.16. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/carla-simulator/scenario_runner

## Chinese / Asian ecosystem
- **AgiBot Genie Sim (智元 · genie_sim)** — open-source simulation platform for environment reconstruction, scene generalization, data collection and automated evaluation; backends for Isaac Sim PhysX, Isaac Sim Newton, and Kit-free Newton-standalone (cloth/soft-body on the Newton path). Benchmark spans 200+ tasks and 100,000+ scenarios and reports under 10% sim-vs-real discrepancy; v3.2.0 (June 2026) adds a realtime RT engine and Isaac Sim 5.1 support, with 10,000+ hours of synthetic data and 5,140 validated 3D assets. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/AgibotTech/genie_sim
- **InternUtopia (formerly GRUtopia, 桃源)** — InternRobotics / Shanghai AI Lab embodied-AGI research platform on NVIDIA Isaac Sim 4.5.0, providing GRScenes (100k interactive annotated environments over 89 categories), GRResidents LLM-driven NPCs, GRBench benchmarks, and a Gym-compatible env API; v2.2.0 released July 2025. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/InternRobotics/InternUtopia
- **RoboTwin** — bimanual manipulation data generator and benchmark from Chinese research groups, currently at RoboTwin 2.0 (ICML 2026; original CVPR 2025 Highlight, early version Best Paper at ECCV Workshop 2024), ~50 tasks, with an Isaac Lab ("IsaacLab-Arena") integration branch and a public leaderboard. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/RoboTwin-Platform/RoboTwin
- **RoboMIND** — multi-embodiment manipulation benchmark from the Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics with Peking University and BAAI: 107,000 real demonstration trajectories over 479 tasks and 96 object classes, plus 5,000 annotated failure demonstrations, and an Isaac Sim digital-twin environment replicating the real tasks and assets for cheap data collection and evaluation. RSS 2025. Status: maintained (dataset + twin). Source: https://x-humanoid-robomind.github.io/
- **OpenLoong** — open-source humanoid embodied-intelligence framework from Humanoid Robot (Shanghai) Co., spanning a cloud-brain OS layer, an "embodied cerebellum" with imitation learning, RL and whole-body dynamics control, open hardware designs with the EthanCat distributed bus, and datasets/pretrained models. Apache-2.0. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/loongOpen/OpenLoong
- **Lightwheel (光轮智能)** — physical-AI infrastructure company selling a SimReady asset/scene library, EgoSuite egocentric human demonstration data, and RoboFinals, an industrial-grade simulation evaluation platform for VLA and world models. Status: commercial. Source: https://lightwheel.ai/
- **unitree_rl_gym** — Unitree's reference RL pipeline for its own platforms (Go2, G1, H1, H1_2) with an explicit Train → Play → Sim2Sim → Sim2Real workflow across Isaac Gym, MuJoCo and hardware, shipping pretrained models plus Python and C++ deployment. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_rl_gym
