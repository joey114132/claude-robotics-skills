# Robot Perception Landscape — verified snapshot
**Verified: 2026-10-02 (changed entries and link fixes; rest 2026-08-05).** Entries were confirmed against live sources on this date — re-verify anything before relying on it if this snapshot is more than ~6 months old.

## Depth & 3D cameras
- **RealSense D555** — global-shutter stereo depth camera, 87°×58° depth FOV, up to 1280×720 @ 90 fps, built-in IMU, PoE+ (802.3at) over RJ45 up to 100 m, IP65, -20 to 50 °C, first camera on the RealSense Vision SoC V5; native ROS 2 support via SafeDDS (interoperable with Fast DDS). Sold by RealSense, Inc. under license from Intel — RealSense announces it has "completed spin-out from Intel" with a $50M raise, so treat it as an independent vendor, not an Intel product line. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.realsenseai.com/products/d555-poe/ — Source: https://www.realsenseai.com/
- **Orbbec Gemini 335L** — active/passive stereo depth camera, 0.17 m to 20 m+ range (optimal 0.25-6 m), depth up to 1280×800 @ 30 fps, RGB up to 1280×800 @ 60 fps, 95 mm baseline, ≤0.8% spatial precision at 2 m, IP65, Orbbec SDK. Shenzhen, China. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.orbbec.com/products/stereo-vision-camera/gemini-335l/
- **Orbbec Gemini 305** — compact stereo depth camera announced at CES 2026 and built for robotic wrist mounting (eye-in-hand): 4 cm minimum imaging distance, 4-100 cm operating range, 88°×65° depth FOV, 94°×68° color FOV, depth up to 1280×800 @ 30 fps, color up to 1280×800 @ 60 fps, USB 3.0 Type-C, 42×42×23 mm, 68 g; the 305g variant adds a GMSL2 serializer with FAKRA connector. The same launch brought the Gemini 345Lg, an IP67 stereo camera rated -20 to 65 °C. Shenzhen, China. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.orbbec.com/products/stereo-vision-camera/gemini-305/ — Source: https://www.therobotreport.com/orbbec-releases-two-new-gemini-stereo-cameras-for-robotics/
- **Mech-Mind Mech-Eye** — industrial 3D cameras and 3D laser profilers sold with an "Eye-Brain-Hand" software stack (Mech-Vision, Mech-Viz, Mech-DLK deep learning, Mech-MSR measurement), targeted at bin picking, depalletizing/palletizing, machine tending and assembly localization. Offices in Beijing and Shanghai plus Munich, Illinois, Tokyo, Seoul, Hong Kong. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.mech-mind.com/
- **Percipio (图漾)** — industrial 3D camera vendor spanning ToF (T series), dot-pattern structured light (G/F/P series) and fringe-pattern structured light (V/U series), plus RVS low-code vision software; targets robotic picking, parcel sorting, AMRs and autonomous forklifts. Chinese (Jiangsu ICP registration). Status: commercial. Source: https://www.percipio.xyz/

## Calibration toolchain
- **Kalibr** — the reference visual-inertial calibration toolbox: multi-camera intrinsics/extrinsics with non-overlapping FOV, camera-IMU spatial *and temporal* calibration, IMU-to-IMU, and rolling-shutter intrinsics. ROS 1 only (Ubuntu 16.04/18.04/20.04 CI); most recent documented feature update November 2022. Status: upstream is ROS 1-only and in bug-fix-only maintenance (last master commit 2024-03-08; the maintainer said in ethz-asl/kalibr issue #725 that a ROS 2 upgrade "likely won't happen any time soon"); no official ROS 2 path. Unofficial, unvetted community ports exist, e.g. https://github.com/yuhang0131/kalibr_ros2 (README claims a ROS 2 Humble port) and https://github.com/ToyotaResearchInstitute/kalibr (per its issue #725 comment, camera calibration only and not heavily tested), so check their distro and feature coverage before relying on them. Source: https://github.com/ethz-asl/kalibr
- **easy_handeye2** — ROS 2 port of easy_handeye (v0.5.0): samples robot pose and tracker output via tf, solves eye-in-hand or eye-on-base using OpenCV's hand-eye routines (Tsai-Lenz), stores the result and republishes it as a tf transform; can drive the robot through sample poses via MoveIt, and ships an `easy_handeye2_demo` simulator package. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/marcoesposito1988/easy_handeye2
- **image_pipeline** — the ROS 2 perception workhorse: `camera_calibration`, `image_proc`, `depth_image_proc`, `stereo_image_proc`, `image_publisher`, `image_rotate`, `image_view`, `tracetools_image_pipeline`. Actively developed on the rolling branch. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/ros-perception/image_pipeline
- **AprilTag 3** — the fiducial system used for most robot extrinsics and hand-eye targets; families include tagStandard41h12 (recommended default), tagStandard52h13, tagCircle49h12/21h7, tagCustom48h12 and native ArUco families; provides pose estimation from camera intrinsics. The ROS 2 wrapper at github.com/christianrauch/apriltag_ros publishes tag poses to `/tf` plus `apriltag_msgs/AprilTagDetectionArray` with homography, hamming distance and decision margin. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/AprilRobotics/apriltag

## Point clouds & 3D mapping
- **PCL (Point Cloud Library)** — the standard C++ library for 2D/3D point cloud processing: filtering, segmentation, registration, features, keypoints, surface reconstruction, recognition, tracking, visualization. Current release 1.15.1, BSD licensed, CI across Ubuntu/Windows/macOS. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/PointCloudLibrary/pcl
- **Open3D** — C++/Python 3D data library with a parallelized backend: 3D data structures, surface alignment/registration, scene reconstruction, GPU acceleration, and PyTorch/TensorFlow 3D ML support. v0.20.0 (2026-09-16) adds Gaussian Splatting rendering and expanded Intel GPU (SYCL) Tensor pipelines, moves the Open3D-ML Python dependencies to an optional extra (`pip install "open3d[ml]"`), replaces OpenMP with oneAPI TBB for CPU parallelism, and drops macOS x86_64 support (macOS is arm64-only). Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/isl-org/Open3D
- **nvblox** — GPU-accelerated 3D reconstruction producing TSDF, ESDF and a 2D costmap for Nav2 vision-based obstacle avoidance; works from depth cameras and/or 3D LiDAR; benchmarked on RTX 5090/3090, laptop A3000, and Jetson AGX Thor / AGX Orin / Orin Nano. Updated 2026-09-21 for the Isaac ROS 5.0.0 release (latest tag v5.0-0). Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/NVIDIA-ISAAC-ROS/isaac_ros_nvblox
- **Isaac ROS 5.0.0** — NVIDIA's Isaac ROS release of 2026-09-21; moves to ROS 2 Lyrical Luth as the supported distribution, adds the Isaac ROS Buildfarm apt repository for Ubuntu 24.04 (Noble), rebuilds NITROS on Lyrical's `rosidl::Buffer` and CUDA buffer backend and removes the isaac_ros_nitros, isaac_ros_managed_nitros, isaac_ros_pynitros, isaac_ros_nitros_topic_tools and isaac_ros_nitros_type packages (code calling NITROS APIs needs a source-level migration), renames isaac_ros_visual_slam to isaac_ros_cuvslam, and adds isaac_ros_gpu_partitioning. Status: maintained. Source: https://nvidia-isaac-ros.github.io/releases/index.html

## Learned 2D and depth perception
- **Ultralytics YOLO** — YOLO26, YOLO11 and YOLOv8 families (n/s/m/l/x sizes) covering detection, instance and semantic segmentation, classification, pose estimation, tracking and oriented bounding boxes. Dual-licensed AGPL-3.0 / commercial enterprise — the license, not the accuracy, is usually the deciding factor for a product. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics
- **Grounding DINO** — text-prompted open-set object detection taking (image, text) pairs and returning boxes with similarity scores; 48.4-52.5 AP zero-shot on COCO, 57.2-63.0 AP fine-tuned; Apache-2.0; successors Grounding DINO 1.5 and Grounded SAM 2 exist as separate projects. Status: not archived, but no commits since 2024-08-12 and no release beyond v0.1.0-alpha2 (2023-04-07). Source: https://github.com/IDEA-Research/GroundingDINO
- **SAM 2 / SAM 2.1** — promptable visual segmentation across images and video with a streaming memory architecture for real-time inference; four checkpoint sizes (tiny/small/base_plus/large); model checkpoints, demo and training code under Apache 2.0. SAM 2.1 released 2024-09-30. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2
- **SAM 3 / SAM 3.1** — Meta's successor to SAM 2: detects, segments and tracks all instances of an open-vocabulary concept given a short text phrase or exemplars, plus point, box and mask prompts, on images and video; SAM 3.1 Object Multiplex (2026-03-27) adds a shared-memory approach for faster joint multi-object tracking. Needs Python 3.12+, PyTorch 2.7+ and CUDA 12.6+; checkpoints on Hugging Face require an access request. Code and weights are under the SAM License, not Apache-2.0 as for SAM 2, so read it before shipping. Status: maintained (last push 2026-09-18). Source: https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3
- **Depth Anything V2** — monocular depth foundation model (NeurIPS 2024) at 24.8M/97.5M/335.3M params (Giant 1.3B announced); main models output *relative* depth, with separate metric-depth fine-tunes in `metric_depth`. Small is Apache-2.0; Base/Large/Giant are CC-BY-NC-4.0 — non-commercial. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2
- **Depth Anything 3** — ByteDance Seed's successor to V2 (paper, code and models released 2025-11-14): predicts spatially consistent depth, camera pose (extrinsics and intrinsics) and 3D Gaussians from one or many views, with or without known poses; sizes 0.08B to 1.40B parameters. The monocular metric model DA3METRIC-LARGE returns meters only after scaling by the camera focal length (`metric_depth = focal * net_output / 300`, focal in pixels, typically the mean of fx and fy), so wrong intrinsics give wrong scale; the nested any-view model outputs meters directly. Licenses are mixed: Apache-2.0 for DA3-SMALL, DA3-BASE, DA3METRIC-LARGE and DA3MONO-LARGE, CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial) for the Large, Giant and nested models. The README deprecates the original DA3-LARGE, DA3-GIANT and DA3NESTED-GIANT-LARGE checkpoints in favour of the retrained `-1.1` versions. Community ROS 2 wrappers exist. Status: research, maintained (last push 2026-07-27). Source: https://github.com/ByteDance-Seed/Depth-Anything-3
- **FoundationStereo** — zero-shot stereo matching foundation model trained on 1M synthetic stereo pairs with a side-tuned monocular-prior backbone; takes rectified undistorted stereo pairs (RGB, mono or IR) plus intrinsics and baseline for metric depth; CVPR 2025 Oral, Best Paper nomination; commercial version via NVIDIA TAO. Status: research + commercial variant. arXiv:2501.09898. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09898
- **VGGT (Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer)** — feed-forward network inferring camera parameters, point maps, depth maps and 3D point tracks from one to hundreds of views in under a second; state of the art on camera pose, multi-view depth, dense reconstruction and 3D point tracking, and useful as a pretrained backbone. Status: research. arXiv:2503.11651. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11651

## 6D pose estimation
- **FoundationPose** — unified 6D pose estimation *and* tracking for novel objects, model-based (CAD) or model-free (a few reference images), no test-time fine-tuning; ranked #1 on the BOP leaderboard for model-based novel-object pose as of 2024/03. NVIDIA Source Code License (not permissive). Status: maintained research code. Source: https://github.com/NVlabs/FoundationPose — arXiv:2312.08344. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08344
- **SAM-6D** — zero-shot 6D pose from RGB-D using SAM for instance proposals plus a two-stage point-matching stage for dense 3D-3D correspondence; requires a CAD model (.ply) alongside RGB, depth and intrinsics; CVPR 2024; FastSAM and Docker support added 2024-03. Status: research. Source: https://github.com/JiehongLin/SAM-6D — arXiv:2311.15707. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15707
- **Isaac ROS Pose Estimation** — production ROS 2 packages wrapping three methods: FoundationPose (novel objects without retraining, refine + score models), CenterPose (known object *classes*, TAO-supported), DOPE (fastest, known objects only). Runs on AGX Thor T5000/T4000, DGX Spark, x86_64 + RTX 5090 and Jetson; published 5.0 benchmarks show FoundationPose at 720p running 2.42 fps on AGX Thor T5000, 0.746 fps on AGX Orin and 5.16 fps on an x86_64 RTX 5090, while CenterPose reaches 50.2 fps at VGA, so budget under 6 fps for FoundationPose on embedded hardware. Updated 2026-09-21 for Isaac ROS 5.0.0. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/NVIDIA-ISAAC-ROS/isaac_ros_pose_estimation
- **BOP Challenge** — the benchmark that defines the field, running since 2017. BOP 2025 covers 11 tracks over BOP-Classic-Core, BOP-H3 (AR/VR, model-free detection) and the new BOP-Industrial bin-picking datasets XYZ-IBD, ITODD-MV and IPD, with both single-view and multi-view evaluation. For historical calibration, the organizers report model-based 6D localization of *seen* objects improving from 56.9 to 86.0 AR on the seven core datasets between 2017 and 2024. Status: maintained benchmark. Source: https://bop.felk.cvut.cz/challenges/ — arXiv:2403.09799. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.09799

## Odometry, VIO & SLAM
- **cuVSLAM / isaac_ros_cuvslam (formerly isaac_ros_visual_slam)** — NVIDIA-accelerated stereo visual-inertial odometry and SLAM taking one or more stereo cameras plus an optional IMU; reported 0.007 s runtime with 0.94% translation error and 0.0019 deg/m rotation error on KITTI (measured on Jetson AGX Xavier). Isaac ROS 5.0.0 renamed the package from isaac_ros_visual_slam to isaac_ros_cuvslam (isaac_ros_visual_slam_interfaces is unchanged) and moved the stack to ROS 2 Lyrical; updated 2026-09-21. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/NVIDIA-ISAAC-ROS/isaac_ros_visual_slam — Source: https://nvidia-isaac-ros.github.io/releases/index.html
- **OpenVINS** — MSCKF sliding-window visual-inertial estimator from UD's RPNG, supporting mono/stereo/binocular, five feature representations, and online calibration of camera-IMU spatial *and temporal* offsets, camera intrinsics and inertial intrinsics; ROS 1, ROS 2 and no-ROS builds with Docker images. Latest release v2.7 (May 2023). Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/rpng/open_vins
- **ORB-SLAM3** — first real-time library combining visual, visual-inertial and multi-map SLAM; monocular, stereo and RGB-D with pin-hole and fisheye models. GPLv3 (commercial licensing on request) — a real constraint for products. V1.0 released 2021-12-22. Status: research, largely static. Source: https://github.com/UZ-SLAMLab/ORB_SLAM3
- **FAST-LIO2** — tightly-coupled iterated-EKF LiDAR-inertial odometry running on raw points with no feature extraction, using a parallel KD-tree; supports Velodyne, Ouster and Livox (Avia, Horizon, MID-70) with optional external IMU; requires ROS ≥ Melodic (ROS 1) on the `main` branch, PCL ≥ 1.8, Eigen ≥ 3.3.4; a separate `ROS2` branch (README: "ROS2 Fork repo maintainer: Ericsii") targets ROS ≥ Foxy (Humble recommended) with livox_ros_driver2. For Livox LiDARs only the driver's CustomMsg format works, because it carries the per-point timestamps that motion undistortion needs. Status: research, widely deployed; last commit on `main` 2024-07-23, on the `ROS2` branch 2025-01-15. Source: https://github.com/hku-mars/FAST_LIO
- **rtabmap_ros** — appearance-based RGB-D / stereo / 3D-LiDAR graph SLAM with loop closure; ROS 2 Humble minimum, with Humble, Jazzy and Rolling builds; BSD-3-Clause. The pragmatic "works out of the box" choice for RGB-D mapping. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/introlab/rtabmap_ros
- **MASt3R-SLAM** — real-time dense monocular SLAM built on the MASt3R two-view reconstruction prior; no fixed or parametric camera model assumed beyond a unique camera centre; ~15 FPS with globally consistent poses and dense geometry, state of the art with known calibration. Status: research. arXiv:2412.12392. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12392

## Fusion, sync & timing
- **robot_localization** — nonlinear state estimation nodes (EKF and UKF) from Charles River Analytics fusing odometry, IMU and GPS via `navsat_transform`; the default ROS answer for pose fusion. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/cra-ros-pkg/robot_localization
- **fuse** — plugin-based nonlinear least-squares sensor fusion on Ceres Solver: sensors publish constraints, motion models link states across timestamps, an optimizer solves the graph. Handles asynchronous sensors and doubles as a calibration framework — the structural alternative to a filter when measurements arrive out of order. Status: active development (devel branch). Source: https://github.com/locusrobotics/fuse
- **message_filters** — the ROS 2 package implementing message synchronizers and filters; maintained on the rolling branch. This is where ExactTime/ApproximateTime sync policies live, and where most "my RGB and depth don't match" bugs get fixed. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/ros2/message_filters
- **linuxptp** — IEEE 1588 Precision Time Protocol implementation for Linux, hosted and supported by the Network Time Foundation; the standard route to sub-microsecond clock alignment across multiple sensor hosts. Status: maintained. Source: https://linuxptp.nwtime.org/
