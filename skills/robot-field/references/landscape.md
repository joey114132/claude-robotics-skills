# Field & Outdoor Robotics Landscape — verified snapshot

**Verified: 2026-08-05.** Entries were confirmed against live sources on this date — re-verify anything before relying on it if this snapshot is more than ~6 months old.

## GNSS / RTK positioning

- **u-blox ZED-F9P** — multi-band GNSS module with built-in RTK; reaches 1–2 cm accuracy with corrections from a local base or an NTRIP/VRS service, accepts RTCM 3.3 or SPARTN 2.0, and typically converges to fix in under two minutes under open sky. Status: commercial (maintained product line). Source: https://www.u-blox.com/en/product/zed-f9p-module
- **Swift Navigation Skylark** — cloud GNSS corrections service in three tiers: Nx RTK (1–2 cm, network RTK/VRS), Cx (3–7 cm, PPP-RTK, OSR and SSR formats), Dx (sub-metre DGNSS); carrier-operated reference network, hardware-agnostic, NTRIP delivery. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.swiftnav.com/skylark
- **ntrip_client (LORD MicroStrain)** — ROS node that connects to an NTRIP caster and republishes RTCM corrections on a ROS topic (`mavros_msgs/RTCM` or `rtcm_msgs/Message`), with NMEA uplink for VRS services; separate ROS 1 (Melodic/Noetic) and ROS 2 branches — the ROS 2 branch documents Foxy/Galactic, so check it against your distro. Status: maintained (community activity; distro coverage lags). Source: https://github.com/LORD-MicroStrain/ntrip_client
- **robot_localization** — nonlinear state estimation (EKF/UKF) nodes for fusing IMU, wheel odometry and GNSS; the standard glue for GNSS-plus-dead-reckoning field odometry. Active development on `rolling-devel`. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/cra-ros-pkg/robot_localization

## Off-road localization & mapping (no prior map)

- **FAST-LIO / FAST-LIO2** — tightly-coupled LiDAR-inertial odometry with an iterated Kalman filter and direct scan-to-map registration on raw points; supports Velodyne, Ouster and Livox (Avia/Horizon/MID-70); ROS ≥ Melodic; GPL-2.0. The default off-road LIO baseline. Status: maintained (low commit cadence, very widely used). Source: https://github.com/hku-mars/FAST_LIO
- **GLIM** — factor-graph range-based 3D mapping with voxelized GICP and optional GPU acceleration; spinning, non-repetitive and solid-state LiDAR plus RGB-D; ROS 1 and ROS 2 packages, Docker images, tested on CUDA 12.x/13.1 and Jetson Orin; MIT. Latest release v1.2.2 (2026-07-12). Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/koide3/glim
- **KISS-ICP** — parameter-free point-to-point ICP LiDAR odometry that holds up in unstructured environments; ROS 2 supported, ROS 1 deprecated after v0.3.0; MIT. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/PRBonn/kiss-icp
- **elevation_mapping_cupy** — GPU robot-centric elevation mapping with a learning-based traversability filter, raycast artifact removal, drift compensation and visibility cleanup; fuses geometry, semantics and RGB into one map. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/leggedrobotics/elevation_mapping_cupy

## Off-road perception datasets & benchmarks

- **GOOSE (German Outdoor and Offroad Dataset)** — ~15,000 annotated image and point-cloud frames from unstructured off-road scenes, with RGB, NIR, multi-LiDAR and localization data; CC BY-SA 4.0; Fraunhofer IOSB, UniBW Munich, University of Koblenz; releases since 2023 with community challenges running 2025–2026. Status: maintained. Source: https://goose-dataset.de/
- **WildScenes** — bi-modal (camera + LiDAR) natural-forest dataset from two Australian parks: 21 km / 300 min of traversal, 9,306 annotated images and 12,148 annotated point clouds with 6-DoF poses and fine-grained vegetation classes (foliage vs trunk, dirt vs mud). IJRR 2024. Status: maintained. Source: https://csiro-robotics.github.io/WildScenes/
- **RELLIS-3D** — off-road multimodal dataset from non-paved trails at Texas A&M's Rellis campus; 13,556 LiDAR scans with point-wise labels over 20 classes. Status: research (widely used baseline). arXiv:2011.12954. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.12954
- **STONE** — large-scale surround-view 3D traversability dataset: 128-channel LiDAR, six RGB cameras and three 4D imaging radars, day and night, across grassland, farmland, construction sites and lakes; annotation-free auto-labelling pipeline plus a voxel-level 3D traversability benchmark. Status: research. arXiv:2603.09175. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09175
- **Rosario Dataset v2** — 2+ hours of soybean-field data with stereo IR and colour cameras, IMU, wheel odometry and GNSS in SPP, RTK and PPK modes, hardware-synchronized with 6-DoF ground truth; built to expose SLAM failures under natural lighting, motion blur and perceptual aliasing in crops. Status: research. arXiv:2508.21635. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21635

## Traversability & off-road navigation research

- **TRAIL (implicit neural traversability)** — models terrain properties as a continuous implicit field whose spatial gradients drive gradient-based trajectory optimization over both path geometry and speed profile, addressing the short look-ahead of sampling planners like MPPI. Status: research. arXiv:2511.18183. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18183
- **Traversability-aware global planners from overhead data** — learns a continuous traversability map from satellite/aerial imagery supervised by human GPS trajectories plus self-supervised LiDAR priors; released dataset of 299 scenes over ~1,244 km² with 1,130 km of human driving; field trials on a Clearpath Warthog cut operator interventions ~85% versus local-planner-only autonomy. Status: research. arXiv:2607.23743. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23743
- **Tree-SLAM** — semantic object SLAM that maps individual orchard trees by detecting and re-identifying trunks as landmarks in a factor graph with noisy GPS and odometry; ~18 cm geo-localization error under canopy where GPS is unreliable. Status: research. arXiv:2507.12093. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12093
- **Offroad-Nav (open-source LiDAR + monocular stack)** — full off-road navigation stack where zero-shot monocular depth (Depth Anything V2) rescaled by sparse SLAM matches high-resolution LiDAR in most scenarios, feeding a robot-centric 2.5D elevation map for costmap planning; code and simulation environment released. Status: research (open source). arXiv:2604.03096. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03096
- **RAMBA** — bundle adjustment for 4D-radar mapping (covariance-weighted GICP residuals, IMU preintegration, ego-velocity constraints, loop closures) — relevant where dust, rain or fog degrade LiDAR. Status: research. arXiv:2605.25041. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25041

## Field robot platforms

- **Clearpath Warthog** — large all-terrain UGV: 272 kg payload, 18 km/h, brief water locomotion, low ground pressure for soft soil, vegetation, mud and steep grades; runtime is short — 2.5 h lead-acid or 3 h Li-ion, so plan charging around it; ships with ROS 2 Jazzy and a Gazebo model. Status: commercial. Source: https://clearpathrobotics.com/warthog-unmanned-ground-vehicle-robot/
- **Bonsai Robotics / Amiga (formerly farm-ng)** — farm-ng.com now redirects to bonsairobotics.ai; the Amiga modular field robot line is marketed as "powered by Bonsai Intelligence", alongside OEM autonomy integrations (e.g. the OMC AR-500 sprayer). Treat older farm-ng documentation as possibly orphaned. Status: commercial. Source: https://bonsairobotics.ai/
- **ANYbotics ANYmal X** — explosion-proof legged inspection robot, ATEX and IECEx certified to Zone 1 IIB, IP67, with thermal (-10 to +400 °C), zoom, acoustic, gas and LiDAR payloads for oil, gas and chemical sites. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.anybotics.com/robotics/anymal-x/
- **OpenMower** — open-source conversion of cheap consumer robotic mowers to RTK-GPS-guided ROS robots (custom mainboards, xESC motor controllers, custom Linux image); roughly €700 of hardware excluding the donor mower and the RTK base. The cheapest realistic way to get hands-on with RTK field autonomy. Status: maintained (active community). Source: https://github.com/ClemensElflein/OpenMower

## Agriculture: guidance, implements & commercial systems

- **AgOpenGPS** — open-source precision-agriculture guidance: real-time field mapping, auto-steer, AB line / curve / contour modes, auto headland turning and up to 64-section implement control, with AgIO as the hardware communication hub; Arduino-based hardware interface and NMEA GPS input. Status: maintained (active farmer community). Source: https://horizon-openagri.eu/open-source-catalogue/agopengps/
- **AgIsoStack++** — free C++11 ISO 11783 (ISOBUS) stack covering virtual terminal client, task controller and auxiliary controls, plus NMEA 2000 and J1939, with drivers for several CAN controllers; MIT licensed. The realistic path to talking to commercial implements. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/Open-Agriculture/AgIsoStack-plus-plus
- **ArduPilot Rover** — open-source autopilot firmware for ground and water vehicles, extending beyond 3/4-wheel platforms to boats, sailboats, balance bots and walking robots, with autonomous mission execution from mission-planning software. Status: maintained (docs page carried a 2024 copyright at fetch time — confirm the current release). Source: https://ardupilot.org/rover/
- **Carbon Robotics LaserWeeder G2 / Carbon Autonomy** — deep-learning weed identification with laser kill: 30 × 150 W diode lasers, 5,000+ weeds per minute, 0.5–1.5 acres/hour, owned and operated by 100+ growers across North America, Europe and Australia. Perception runs on a "Large Plant Model" trained on 150 M labelled plants across 15 countries and 100+ crops; the autonomy product is *Carbon Autonomy*, a retrofit kit for John Deere 6R/8R/8RX/8RT (2019+) tractors with no permanent modification — the reference example of retrofit-over-new-platform. Status: commercial. Source: https://carbonrobotics.com/laserweeder and https://carbonrobotics.com/
- **Naïo Technologies** — French specialty-crop robots: Oz (market gardening), Ted (vineyard straddler), Jo (narrow-vineyard crawler) and Orio (tool carrier), with an "Augmented Autonomy" mode marketed as fleet operation without in-field supervision; 40 certified partners in 20+ countries. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.naio-technologies.com/en/
- **ros-agriculture (GitHub org)** — historic ROS 1 agricultural packages (ublox_f9p driver, ntrip_ros, tractor_localization, gps_goal_server, lawn_tractor). Most repositories were last touched around 2021 and some are archived — useful as reference code, not as a dependency. Status: unmaintained. Source: https://github.com/ros-agriculture

- **XAG (极飞科技)** — Chinese agricultural robotics vendor. Its product page lists agricultural drones (P200, P150 Ultra and P120 for 2026; P150 Pro and P60 Pro for 2025), unmanned ground vehicles (RM80 2026, R100 and R200 2025), survey drones (M2000, M500), APC-series autopilot kits and smart-farm IoT valves and fertigation. Chinese-language site; it timed out for the automated link checker but loads in a browser, so verify by hand rather than trusting a link check. Status: commercial. Source: https://xa.com
- **Chinese autonomous mining haulage market** — over 4,000 autonomous mining trucks running nationwide by end-2025, unmanned-truck sales penetration up from 1.1% (2021) to 12.1% (2025), and the domestic solutions market projected to reach RMB 22.3 bn by 2030; Yikong Zhijia (易控智驾, HKEX 07687) opened its Hong Kong IPO subscription on 2026-06-29. Open-pit haulage is one of the few off-road autonomy applications already deployed at commercial fleet scale — worth studying before designing a new one. Status: commercial. Source: https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/newstock/2026-07-01/doc-inifiaeu6245599.shtml

## Simulation for terrain & field

- **Project Chrono (Chrono::Vehicle)** — BSD-3 multi-physics engine with template-based wheeled and tracked vehicle models, tire and powertrain models, and explicit terramechanics: deformable soil and granular flows for off-road mobility. The reference choice when wheel-terrain interaction, sinkage and slip matter more than photorealism. Status: maintained (University of Wisconsin-Madison and University of Parma). Source: https://projectchrono.org/
- **OrchardBench** — GPU-parallel apple-orchard simulation on the Newton engine with L-system-grown, physically articulated trees (compliant branches with Euler-Bernoulli stiffness, rupture, apples on stem tethers with literature-grounded detach forces) and a mobile-manipulator harvesting baseline that clears only about an eighth of reachable fruit. Status: research. arXiv:2607.06337. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06337
- **Verti-Bench** — off-road *mobility* benchmark rather than a static dataset: 100 environments and 1,000 navigation tasks on vertically challenging terrain in multi-physics simulation (built on Chrono), with rigid and deformable surfaces, expert and failure datasets, and a gym-like RL interface. Status: research. arXiv:2502.11426. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11426
