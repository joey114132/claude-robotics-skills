# Robot Safety & Compliance Landscape — verified snapshot
**Verified: 2026-08-05.** Entries were confirmed against live sources on this date — re-verify anything before relying on it if this snapshot is more than ~6 months old.

## Robot-specific safety standards
- **ISO 10218-1:2025** — Robotics — Safety requirements — Part 1: Industrial robots (duties of the robot manufacturer); on sale from February 2025. Where the 2011 edition required a handful of safety functions the 2025 edition requires more than twenty, adds new robot classifications with matching functional-safety requirements and test methods, and adds cybersecurity requirements as they bear on robot safety. The 2011 edition is to be withdrawn in spring 2027, so files citing it have a deadline. Status: maintained. Source: https://www.therobotreport.com/iso-10218-industrial-robot-safety-standard-receives-major-overhaul/ and https://www.universal-robots.com/blog/safer-clearer-and-more-explicit-iso-10218-gets-a-makeover/
- **ISO 10218-2:2025** — Part 2: Industrial robot applications and robot cells (duties of the integrator); on sale from February 2025. Absorbs most of the collaborative-application content formerly in ISO/TS 15066 and the end-effector guidance formerly in ISO/TR 20218; treats collaboration as a property of the *application*, not the robot. Status: maintained. Source: https://theresarobotforthat.com/blog/iso-10218-2025-explained/
- **ISO/TS 15066:2016** — Collaborative robots technical specification; the four collaborative modes and the biomechanical contact-limit tables. Its content is now normatively integrated into ISO 10218:2025 rather than cited standalone. Status: superseded in practice — verify before citing. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17822
- **ANSI/A3 R15.06-2025** — US national adoption of ISO 10218-1:2025 and -2:2025 in full, plus a US/Canada-developed Part 3 on use of industrial robot cells. Parts 1-2 approved 21 Aug 2025, Part 3 approved 7 Oct 2025, published 29 Oct 2025; 403 pages; replaces ANSI/RIA R15.06-2012. Renames "safety-rated monitored stop" to "monitored standstill". Status: maintained. Source: https://www.therobotreport.com/now-available-full-403-page-ansi-a3-r15-06-2025-robot-safety-standard/ (dates, page count, replacement) and https://theresarobotforthat.com/blog/iso-10218-2025-explained/ (the monitored-standstill renaming)

## Machinery functional-safety foundations (Type A/B standards)
- **ISO 12100:2010** — Safety of machinery: general principles for design, risk assessment and risk reduction. The root method every robot risk assessment inherits; still the current edition, cross-checked through its identical (IDT) Chinese adoption GB/T 15706-2012. Status: maintained. Source: https://www.chinesestandard.net (catalogue — search GB/T 15706; iso.org blocks automated fetches)
- **ISO 13849-1:2023** — Safety-related parts of control systems, Performance Levels PL a-e. EN version cited in the EU Official Journal May 2024 with a transition period to 15 May 2027; changes the methodology for determining the required level and adds application-software requirements by complexity. Status: maintained. Source: https://machinerysafety101.com/2023/05/01/do-not-use-iso-13849-1-2023/
- **IEC 62061:2021 (+AMD1:2024)** — Functional safety of safety-related control systems using SIL 1-3; scoped to electrical/electronic/programmable-electronic systems, harmonised alongside ISO 13849-1. Status: maintained. Source: https://61508.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/09A-T6A-Symposium-Presentation-IEC-62061-and-ISO-13849.pdf
- **IEC 61508 Edition 2.0 (2010)** — the generic functional-safety umbrella for E/E/PE safety-related systems; the parent from which SIL derives and the fallback where no sector standard exists. Status: maintained. Source: https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/5515
- **ISO 13855:2024** — Positioning of safeguards with respect to the approach of the human body; published November 2024, replacing the now-withdrawn ISO 13855:2010. Sets the separation distances for electro-sensitive protective equipment, pressure-sensitive devices, two-hand controls and interlocking guards, for persons aged 14 and over. Status: maintained. Source: https://genorma.com/en/standards/iso-13855-2024 (iso.org/standard/80590.html blocks automated fetches)
- **IEC 61800-5-2** — Functional safety for power drive systems; defines the drive-level safety functions used in robot joints and mobile drivetrains: STO (Safe Torque Off), SS1 (Safe Stop 1), SLS (Safe Limited Speed), SLP (Safe Limited Position). Status: maintained. Source: https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/24556

## Mobile and service robot safety standards
- **ISO 3691-4:2023** — Industrial trucks — safety requirements — Part 4: driverless industrial trucks and their systems. Covers AGVs, AMRs, tuggers and carts; normative Annex A on preparing operating zones, Annex B hazard catalogue. A revision is in ballot as ISO/DIS 3691-4 — stage 40.20, 12-week DIS ballot closing 17 June 2026 — so check the edition before quoting a clause. Status: maintained, in revision. Source: https://www.iso.org/standard/83545.html and https://genorma.com/en/standards/iso-dis-3691-4 (DIS stage and ballot date; iso.org blocks automated fetches)
- **ANSI/A3 R15.08-1-2020** — US safety requirements for industrial mobile robots (IMRs), Part 1: the IMR itself; published December 2020; introduces the IMR Type A/B/C classification (Type A = mobile platform only). Status: maintained. Source: https://www.automate.org/robotics/industry-insights/mobile-robot-standard-r15-08-1-2020-what-you-need-to-know
- **ANSI/A3 R15.08-2-2023** — Part 2: requirements for IMR systems and IMR applications — integrating, configuring and customising a robot or fleet into a site. Status: maintained. Source: https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/ria/ansia3r15082023
- **ISO 13482:2014** — Safety requirements for personal care robots; the only published international standard aimed at non-industrial service robots. A revision is circulating as ISO/DIS 13482. Status: maintained, in revision. Source: https://www.iso.org/standard/53820.html
- **ISO 25785-1 (under development)** — safety requirements for industrial mobile robots with *actively controlled stability*; the scope names quadrupedal, bipedal and wheeled balancing robots, and excludes robots under direct human control. Very early: stage 10.99 "new project approved" as of 9 January 2026, no publication date set — do not plan a conformity route around it. Status: draft. Source: https://genorma.com/en/standards/pren-iso-25785-1

## Regulation and certification routes
- **Regulation (EU) 2023/1230** — the EU Machinery Regulation of 14 June 2023, repealing Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC and Directive 73/361/EEC; applies from 20 January 2027 with deferred application to give operators transition time. Adds cybersecurity, AI-related and lifecycle requirements relative to the old directive. Status: in force, applies 2027. Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/oj
- **UL 3100** — Automated Mobile Platforms (AMPs): battery-operated mobile platforms with or without payload for indoor/outdoor commercial and industrial use; evaluates battery safety, obstacle detection and control fail-safes. Edition 1 dated 2021 with subsequent revisions; a further revision is in circulation. Status: maintained. Source: https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/ul/ul3100ed2021
- **UL 3300** — Service, Communication, Information, Education and Entertainment (SCIEE) robots: public-facing service robots, with test focus on human-robot interaction safety and untrained users including children. Status: maintained. Source: https://www.hardwarecompliance.com/blog/ul-certification-robotics-standard-does-robot-actually-need

## China standards and certification ecosystem
- **GB 11291.1-2011** — 工业环境用机器人 安全要求 第1部分：机器人 (industrial robot safety requirements, Part 1: the robot); published 12 May 2011, effective 1 Oct 2011; replaced GB 11291-1997. Its companion GB 11291.2-2013 (机器人系统与集成, published 17 Dec 2013, effective 1 Nov 2014) covers systems and integration. Mandatory (GB) standards under SAC/TC 159. Status: current, but both track the ISO 10218:2011 generation, not the 2025 edition. Source: https://openstd.samr.gov.cn (national standards registry — search the GB number; deep links are session-bound and the site blocks automated fetches)
- **GB/T 36008-2018** — 机器人与机器人装备 协作机器人 (collaborative robots); published 15 March 2018, effective 1 Oct 2018; corresponds to ISO/TS 15066:2016 and supplements ISO 10218-1:2011 / GB 11291.2-2013. Status: current, recommended (GB/T). Source: https://openstd.samr.gov.cn (registry — search the GB/T number)
- **GB/T 15706-2012** — 机械安全 设计通则 风险评估与风险减小; identical adoption (IDT) of ISO 12100:2010; published 5 Nov 2012, effective 1 Mar 2013. The Chinese entry point for robot risk assessment. Status: current. Source: https://www.chinesestandard.net (catalogue — search the GB/T number)
- **CR certification (中国机器人认证)** — China's unified robot safety, EMC and performance certification scheme run by the China Robot Testing and Certification alliance (机器人检测认证联盟), with core evaluation centres in Shanghai, Shenyang, Guangzhou, Chongqing, Beijing and Wuhu. Covers industrial, service, logistics and collaborative robots plus key components and system integration, and functions as the domestic market credential. Status: regulatory/commercial. Source: https://www.china-tcar.com

## Safety hardware
- **SICK microScan3** — safety laser scanner family, the common choice for AMR protective fields and cell perimeter monitoring. Take the type rating (IEC 61496) and PL from the specific variant's datasheet, not from the family page — they differ across Core/Pro and EFI-pro variants. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.sick.com/us/en/catalog/products/safety/safety-laser-scanners/microscan3/c/g358643 (JavaScript-rendered; open in a browser to read specs)
- **Synapticon safety drives** — servo drives implementing IEC 61800-5-2 functions (STO with hardware blocking of the power stage, SBC, SS1/SS2/SOS); relevant when the safety function has to live in the joint rather than in a cell controller. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.synapticon.com/en/motion-control-academy/safe-torque-off-sto-safe-brake-control-sbc

## Analysis, validation and runtime safety tools
- **SISTEMA (IFA/DGUV)** — free Windows tool that models a safety function's designated architecture and computes the achieved Performance Level per EN ISO 13849-1, with maintained component libraries. The de facto evidence artefact for PL verification. Status: maintained, free. Source: https://www.dguv.de/ifa/praxishilfen/practical-solutions-machine-safety/software-sistema/index.jsp
- **GTE CoboSafe** — modular force and pressure measurement system for inspecting transient and quasi-static contact on collaborative robots, with spring constants matched to body regions and evaluation software for the biomechanical limits. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.gte.de/en/messtechnik/kraft-druckmesssystem-cobosafe
- **Nav2 Collision Monitor** — open-source ROS 2 node that sits between the velocity controller output and the base, slowing or stopping from raw sensor data independently of costmaps and planners. Genuinely useful risk reduction; not a safety-rated device. Status: maintained. Source: https://docs.nav2.org/configuration/packages/configuring-collision-monitor.html

## Research (arXiv)
- **Evolution of Safety Requirements in Industrial Robotics: ISO 10218-1/2 (2011 vs. 2025) and Integration of ISO/TS 15066** — clause-level comparative analysis of the structural, terminological and technical changes, including the new robot/collaborative-application classifications and the cybersecurity expansion. The most useful single map of what actually changed. Status: research. arXiv:2602.17822. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17822
- **Safe physical HRI: toward a unified treatment of speed and separation monitoring together with power and force limiting** — deploys both collaborative regimes in one application and measures the performance of transitions between them; KUKA LBR iiwa + RGB-D skeleton tracking. Status: research. arXiv:1908.03046. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.03046
- **Analysis of Deep-Learning Methods in an ISO/TS 15066-Compliant Human-Robot Safety Framework** — compares body recognition, segmentation, pose estimation and body-part segmentation for distance-adaptive speed control; reports up to 15% cycle-time reduction versus conventional safety technology. Status: research. arXiv:2511.19094. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19094
- **A Verification Methodology for Safety Assurance of Robotic Autonomous Systems** — full-lifecycle workflow from hazard analysis and risk assessment through a formal model of the safety controller to runtime verification, demonstrated on an agricultural field robot. Status: research. arXiv:2506.19622. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19622
- **CBF-RL: Safety Filtering Reinforcement Learning in Training with Control Barrier Functions** — enforces barrier constraints during training so the learned policy internalises them, validated on a Unitree G1 humanoid without a runtime filter. Relevant to the "learned policies have no Performance Level" problem. Status: research. arXiv:2510.14959. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14959
