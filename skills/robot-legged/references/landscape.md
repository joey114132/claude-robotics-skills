# Legged Robotics Landscape — verified snapshot
**Verified: 2026-08-05.** Entries were confirmed against live sources on this date — re-verify anything before relying on it if this snapshot is more than ~6 months old.

## Locomotion control frameworks (MPC / WBC / classic balance)
- **OCS2** — C++ toolbox for optimal control of switched systems (SLQ and multiple-shooting SQP solvers); the base of ETH RSL's MPC + whole-body-control stack for ANYmal and other legged robots. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/leggedrobotics/ocs2
- **Crocoddyl** — DDP-family optimal control library for multi-contact trajectory optimization, built on Pinocchio; widely used for legged/humanoid whole-body MPC research. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/loco-3d/crocoddyl
- **Pinocchio** — fast rigid-body dynamics library (forward/inverse dynamics + analytical derivatives) underlying most modern legged and humanoid WBC stacks, including Crocoddyl. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/stack-of-tasks/pinocchio
- **DIAL-MPC** — sampling-based (MPPI + diffusion-style annealing) full-order, torque-level MPC for legged robots; training-free, no reduced-order model needed; ICRA 2025 best-paper finalist. Status: research (open-source code). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15610 (code: https://github.com/LeCAR-Lab/dial-mpc)
- **Capture point / ZMP balance control** — the classic reduced-order balance criteria: zero-moment-point + support-polygon reasoning for slow flat-ground walking, capture point (extrapolated center of mass) for push recovery and step timing. Still the default for industrial bipeds and still used as the deployable-observation basis in 2026 RL balance research. Status: research/classic. Source: https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/19/15/3407

## RL locomotion toolkits & sim environments
- **Isaac Lab** — NVIDIA's unified, GPU-accelerated robot-learning framework built on Isaac Sim; successor to Isaac Gym with 30+ ready-to-train legged environments (quadruped and humanoid). Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab
- **Isaac Gym / IsaacGymEnvs** — NVIDIA's original GPU-parallel physics + RL environment suite; NVIDIA now directs users to Isaac Lab, so this repo receives limited updates. Status: legacy (receiving minimal updates). Source: https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacGymEnvs
- **legged_gym** — ETH RSL's Isaac Gym-based quadruped/humanoid RL training environment; companion code to "Learning to Walk in Minutes Using Massively Parallel Deep Reinforcement Learning" (Rudin et al., trains ANYmal flat-terrain policies in under 4 minutes on one GPU). Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/leggedrobotics/legged_gym ; paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.11978
- **RSL-RL** — ETH RSL's fast, GPU-native PPO/RL library; backend for legged_gym, mjlab (MuJoCo Warp), and MuJoCo Playground training. Status: maintained. Source: https://leggedrobotics.github.io/rsl_rl/
- **MuJoCo Playground** — Google DeepMind's GPU-accelerated (MJX/Warp) suite of robot-learning environments for sim-to-real transfer, including quadruped and bipedal/humanoid locomotion tasks. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_playground
- **Genesis** — high-throughput (reported ~43M FPS simulating a Franka arm on one RTX 4090) multi-physics simulation platform for general-purpose robot learning; supports quadruped and humanoid locomotion RL. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/Genesis-Embodied-AI/genesis-world
- **RaiSim** — ETH RSL-spun-off rigid-body physics engine tuned for high-fidelity contact dynamics, used in legged-robot RL research requiring accurate sim-to-real transfer. Status: maintained (RaiSim Tech Inc., commercial license for some uses). Source: https://github.com/leggedrobotics/raisimLib

## Quadruped platforms — global
- **Boston Dynamics Spot** — commercial industrial-inspection quadruped; electric actuation, patrols/scans construction sites and factories. Starting price ~$74,500 (2026). Status: commercial. Source: https://standardbots.com/blog/spot-robot
- **ANYbotics ANYmal / ANYmal X** — industrial inspection quadruped, incl. ATEX/IECEx explosive-atmosphere-certified X variant; 1 m/s travel, 20° slopes, 45° stairs, 25 cm gap crossing, 60 cm passage width, ~30 kg base weight, 2–4 h battery, LiDAR-based SLAM. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.blackscarab.ai/insights/anybotics-anymal-industrial-inspection-robot-guide
- **MIT Mini Cheetah** — open, low-cost (~9 kg, ~0.3 m tall) research quadruped with backdriveable modular actuators for high-bandwidth force control; origin platform for convex-MPC gaits and the 360° backflip demo, still an active reference platform in legged-control research. Status: research platform. Source: https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Mini-Cheetah:-A-Platform-for-Pushing-the-Limits-of-Katz-Carlo/bb7e50d5d25ebf46f04c6d8cabdaac72a0e9d297

Status: commercial. Source: https://robotsguide.com/robots/cyberdog2

## Whole-body control / loco-manipulation software
- **Pinocchio + Crocoddyl** — the dominant open-source pairing for model-based humanoid/quadruped whole-body trajectory optimization and MPC (see entries above); most academic loco-manipulation stacks build on this combination. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/stack-of-tasks/pinocchio, https://github.com/loco-3d/crocoddyl
- **awesome-loco-manipulation** — curated, actively updated reading list covering model-based control/planning, whole-body MPC, multi-contact planning, and learning-based loco-manipulation approaches for legged robots. Status: maintained (community list). Source: https://github.com/aCodeDog/awesome-loco-manipulation
- **RTWholeBodyMPPI** — first reported real-time deployment of whole-body sampling-based MPC (MPPI) for contact-rich locomotion *and* manipulation from a single policy on a real quadruped (Unitree Go1). Status: research (open-source code). Source: https://github.com/jrapudg/RTWholeBodyMPPI

## Notable recent research directions (arXiv)
- **RMA: Rapid Motor Adaptation for Legged Robots** (Kumar, Fu, Pathak, Malik, 2021) — sim-trained base policy + adaptation module enabling real-time online adaptation to unseen terrain/payload on the A1 quadruped without fine-tuning; foundational for today's sim-to-real adaptation recipes. Status: research (foundational, still cited). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.04034
- **First Deployable Dynamic-CoM (FDDC)** — puts a hardware-deployable capture-point (extrapolated center-of-mass) observation directly into an on-robot actor for humanoid single-leg balance; shown on a real Unitree G1, plus a new sim2sim single-leg-balance benchmark showing 8 released SOTA policies score 0/90 without this observation. Status: research (2026). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00500
- **ReactiveBFM** — real-time closed-loop planning-control framework giving Behavior Foundation Models reactive, error-recovering whole-body coordination instead of fixed reference-motion playback; deployed on Unitree G1, 93.1% sim-to-sim success under heavy perturbation. Status: research (2026). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30362
- **Athena-WBC** — capability-aligned teacher-student expert pipeline targeting the long-tail of humanoid whole-body control (high-dynamic transitions, balance-critical motions) that default training recipes still fail on. Status: research (2026). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04837
- **VLK (Vision-Language-Kinematics)** — generates synthetic egocentric vision-language-kinematics supervision from 3D-Gaussian-Splat-reconstructed scenes to train perception-based humanoid loco-manipulation, validated on a physical Unitree G1. Status: research (2026). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30645
- **DIAL-MPC** (see also Locomotion control frameworks) — sampling-based full-order torque-level MPC via diffusion-style annealing, training-free legged locomotion control. Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.15610
