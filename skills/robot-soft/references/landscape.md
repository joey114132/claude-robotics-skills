# Soft & Compliant Robotics Landscape — verified snapshot
**Verified: 2026-08-05.** Entries were confirmed against live sources on this date — re-verify anything before relying on it if this snapshot is more than ~6 months old.

## Simulation frameworks (FEM / rod / hybrid)
- **SOFA + SoftRobots plugin** — Inria DEFROST plugin adding cable, pneumatic, and servo actuator models to the SOFA physics framework, with real-time direct *and inverse* FEM solvers so the robot can be commanded in end-effector space while the solver computes actuator inputs. LGPL-3.0. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/SofaDefrost/SoftRobots ; https://www.sofa-framework.org/applications/plugins/softrobots/
- **PyElastica** — Gazzola Lab (UIUC) Python implementation of Elastica; simulates assemblies of slender bodies as Cosserat rods (bend, twist, stretch, shear), Python 3.10+, MIT license, actively developed. A C++ sibling (Elastica++, teraflop-scale rod assemblies) exists, but the lab's own software page calls it "less documented and developed" and recommends PyElastica instead. (Note: that software page predates the May 2026 Elastica++ paper, so check both before choosing.) Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/GazzolaLab/PyElastica ; https://www.cosseratrods.org/software/elastica/ ; Elastica++ paper arXiv:2605.13766
- **DisMech** — discrete-differential-geometry (Discrete Elastic Rods) simulator for elastic rod structures and soft robots, with broad/narrow-phase collision via FCL, per-limb friction, and an implicit contact model. GPL-3.0. Status: maintained. Source: https://github.com/StructuresComp/dismech-rods
- **Py-DiSMech** — Python, fully vectorized DDG simulator for rods, shells, and hybrid structures; penalty-energy implicit contact (rod-rod, rod-shell, shell-shell) and a natural-strain PI shape controller; benchmarked as substantially faster than Elastica at comparable accuracy. Status: research (open source). Source: arXiv:2512.09911
- **SORS (Soft Over Rigid Simulator)** — energy-based FEM simulator with modular material/actuation models and SQP-based constrained contact handling; validated against cantilever deflection, pneumatic soft-arm actuation, and the PokeFlex dataset. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2512.15994
- **Continuum-mechanics-informed MuJoCo discretization** — puts tendon-driven continuum robots natively inside MuJoCo (tendon forces, body contact, dynamics in one pipeline), validated against a Cosserat rod reference and real hardware; used for the first reported sim-to-real transfer of contact-rich continuum manipulation. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2606.22397

## Modeling formulations (reduced-order kinematics/dynamics)
- **Piecewise Affine Curvature (PAC)** — higher-order reduced-order kinematic model that relaxes the constant-curvature hypothesis under external load; reports up to 30% end-position error reduction versus PCC on a soft continuum body. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2211.10188
- **Piecewise Linear Strain (PLS) Cosserat model** — discretization of the Cosserat-rod PDEs for soft slender manipulators, with analytic models and a parameter-identification scheme validated in simulation and experiment. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2206.03546
- **General Lie-group framework for continuum soft robots** — Cosserat rod theory with cumulative parameterization on SE(3); unified kinematics/statics/dynamics, recursive Jacobians, energy-conserving integrator, and extensions to segmented, branched, nested, and rigid-soft composite structures. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2603.08232
- **Quasi-static control of discrete Cosserat (PCS)** — feedback-linearization control laws in strain and task space for Piecewise-Constant-Strain-discretized soft robots, for tip tracking and shape control. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2605.01395
- **Learning-based Cosserat modeling (port-Hamiltonian GP)** — combines Cosserat rod theory with Gaussian-process regression under a port-Hamiltonian structure so the learned model stays energy-consistent and interpretable. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2606.20958

## Actuation & drive hardware
- **Festo DMSP Fluidic Muscle** — the best-known commercial McKibben pneumatic artificial muscle; antagonist pairs allow simultaneous position and stiffness control, but published work notes the difficulty of deriving an accurate static force model across the full pressure range. Status: commercial. Source: arXiv:2104.13167
- **Soft Robotics Toolkit Fluidic Control Board** — open-hardware board for driving fluidic soft actuators (PneuNets bending actuators, fiber-reinforced actuators), published with build documentation as part of the open-access Soft Robotics Toolkit. Status: maintained (open access resource). Source: https://softroboticstoolkit.com/book/control-board (host returns 403 to automated fetches — open in a browser to verify)
- **PneuSoRD** — open-source pneumatic soft-robot driver supporting one pump plus accumulator, 26 on/off valves, 5 proportional valves, and up to 12 sensor inputs in open- or closed-loop. Status: research (open hardware). Source: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9635874/
- **Electronics-free pneumatic control circuits** — soft-legged robot walking driven by fluidic logic instead of electromagnetic valves and microcontrollers; the reference point for untethered, electronics-free pneumatic control. Status: research. Source: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scirobotics.aay2627
- **3D-printed hydraulic fluidic logic circuitry** — printed fluidic logic elements for soft robots, replacing bulky electromagnetic valving with monolithic printed circuits. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2401.16827
- **HASEL / electrohydraulic actuators (review)** — surveyed devices operate between 1.1 kV (HALVE) and 22.5 kV (planar HASEL) with most designs at 8–12 kV; reported strains range from ~9–15% (Peano HASEL) to 118% free strain (quadrant HASEL); maximum blocking forces include 60 N (Peano and quadrant HASEL) and 37.6 N (HEXEL); stated drawbacks are limited force/displacement, dielectric breakdown events, fabrication inconsistency, and high-voltage supply and safety burden. Status: research (review, Biomimetics, March 2025). Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11939893/
- **Artimus Robotics** — commercial supplier of HASEL contracting and expanding actuators plus specialty high-voltage amplifiers for research and OEM use. No specs published on the landing page — request a datasheet before designing to numbers. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.artimusrobotics.com/
- **DEA modeling/control/self-sensing (review)** — dielectric elastomer actuators offer light weight, large strain, fast response, and high energy density, but nonlinear elasticity, viscoelastic creep, hysteresis, and vibrational dynamics make modeling and control hard; the review covers physics-based and phenomenological models, open-loop/feedback/adaptive control, and self-sensing that reconstructs displacement without extra sensors. Status: research (review, 2026). Source: arXiv:2604.17199
- **SMA artificial muscle with active + passive heat dissipation** — addresses the dominant SMA limitation (cooling-bound bandwidth) using thermoelectric modules plus hydrogel evaporation to raise cooling rate. Status: research (Actuators, 2025). Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2076-0825/14/5/248

## Commercial soft grippers & end-effectors
- **OnRobot Soft Gripper** — food-grade soft gripper for cobots; up to 2.2 kg payload (depends on object shape, softness, friction), 11–118 mm grip range across interchangeable star/finger silicone cups, FDA 21 CFR and EC 1935/2004 compliant, dishwasher-safe, and requires no external air supply. Status: commercial. Source: https://onrobot.com/en/products/soft-gripper
- **Piab piSOFTGRIP 40-3** — three-finger, one-piece food-grade silicone soft gripper actuated purely by vacuum (no valves or extra actuators); launched 10 June 2026 as the larger-envelope member of the piSOFTGRIP family, targeting fresh produce, protein, bakery, confectionery, and fragile components. Payload not stated in the launch coverage. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.roboticstomorrow.com/news/2026/06/11/piab-introduces-pisoftgrip-40-3-expanded-soft-gripping-capacity-for-delicate-irregular-products/26705

## Continuum robots & medical systems
- **Intuitive Ion** — robotic bronchoscopy platform with an ultrathin, ultramaneuverable catheter reaching all 18 lung segments; vendor states it is the only bronchoscopy platform using shape sensing, measuring catheter shape "100s of times per second" (the page describes fiber-optic sensing separately, so do not assume the two claims are one). Introduced 2019; more than 100,000 Ion biopsies completed. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.intuitive.com/en-us/products-and-services/ion
- **CoLI** — open-source continuum-robot platform fabricated as a monolithic compliant structure by multi-material 3D printing, with an isomorphic teleoperation interface (direct actuator-level mapping, no explicit kinematic model) and imitation-learning support. Aimed squarely at the reproducibility problem in continuum robotics. Status: research (open source). Source: arXiv:2606.20389
- **Hybrid Hinge-Beam continuum robot with fatigue awareness** — decouples torsion and bending (TwistBeam/BendBeam) to cut fatigue accumulation ~49% versus a conventional design, and estimates structural fatigue online from motor torque at a mechanically limited pose without extra sensors. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2509.09404

## Sensing & shape estimation
- **Liquid metal sensors for soft robots (review)** — survey of liquid-metal-based stretchable sensing for soft-robot proprioception, including capacitive curvature sensors embedded in soft actuators that track deformation without materially stiffening the actuator. Status: research (review, Advanced Robotics Research, 2025). Source: https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adrr.202500057
- **Distributed time-of-flight localization for continuum robots** — small, low-resolution ToF sensors along the body fused with a shape prior; 2.5 cm mean position and 7.2° rotation error on a 53 cm robot across simulation and real environments, despite frequent per-sensor degenerate views. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2602.07209
- **Factor-graph shape estimation via Magnus expansion** — estimates Geometric Variable Strain coefficients inside a factor graph, giving compact control-ready state with probabilistic uncertainty; sub-2 mm mean position error on a simulated 0.4 m tendon-driven continuum robot. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2604.15619
- **Multi-dynamics perception for TDCRs** — unifies motor electrical dynamics, motor-winch dynamics, and continuum dynamics so contact detection and object-size estimation come from intrinsic motor current/position signals instead of added sensors; captures actuation hysteresis and self-contact. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2511.18088

## Control & learning
- **RL in a shared linear Koopman embedding** — configuration-agnostic soft-robot control validated across 33 distinct robot configurations, reporting a 75x reduction in transfer samples with robustness to high-speed motion, heavy payloads, and multi-actuator faults. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2606.08104
- **Velocity-form DeePC under unknown payloads** — data-enabled predictive control in incremental input-output form so unknown external payloads do not produce offset error; validated on a planar soft robot against standard DeePC. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2510.04509
- **Learning-based dynamics + robust control for TDCRs** — GRU dynamics model with residual prediction used as a differentiable gradient bridge to train an end-to-end neural controller that absorbs frictional hysteresis; on a physical three-section TDCR it removes the self-excited oscillations seen with Jacobian-based control. Status: research (code released). Source: arXiv:2604.25691
- **SOFTMAP** — sim-to-real forward modeling for tendon-actuated soft fingers via ARAP topological mesh alignment plus a residual correction network trained on a small real dataset; 0.389 mm Chamfer distance in simulation, 3.786 mm on hardware, at 30 FPS. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2603.19384
- **CR-Solver** — GPU-accelerated, pure-Python optimization solver unifying inverse kinematics, path following, and trajectory planning for tendon-driven continuum robots; >95% success at millimeter accuracy with large speedups over CPU solvers. Status: research. Source: arXiv:2607.11340
