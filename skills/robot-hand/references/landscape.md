# Robot Hand & Dexterous Manipulation Landscape — verified snapshot
**Verified: 2026-10-02 (changed entries and link fixes; teleop/retargeting entries 2026-09-09; rest 2026-08-05).** Entries were confirmed against live sources on this date — re-verify anything before relying on it if this snapshot is more than ~6 months old.

## Parallel-jaw & industrial grippers
- **Robotiq 2F-85** — electric adaptive parallel-jaw gripper, 85 mm stroke, 20–235 N grip force, 5 kg payload, IP40, the most widely deployed research/light-industrial parallel gripper. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.electromate.com/2f-85-and-2f-140-grippers/
- **OnRobot 2FG7** — sealed compact 2-finger parallel gripper, 38 mm stroke, 20–140 N force, 7 kg force-fit / 11 kg form-fit payload, IP67 (ISO Class 5 cleanroom with optional silicone bellows). Status: commercial. Source: https://onrobot.com/en/products/2fg7-finger-gripper

## 3-4 finger dexterous hands
- **Wonik Robotics Allegro Hand V5 / V5 Plus** — the vendor lists V5 as a 9-DOF hand (3 fingers x 3 active joints, DC motor, CAN at 500 Hz, payload 12 kg "depending on the measurement method") and V5 Plus as the 16-DOF four-finger hand (4 fingers x 4 active joints, same CAN 500 Hz, payload 15 kg). Both carry 360° omnidirectional pressure-sensitive fingertip tactile sensors, with four fingertip types (A-D) sold separately. A four-finger Allegro in the papers below means the 16-joint hand (V4 or V5 Plus), not the 9-DOF V5. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.allegrohand.com/sub/product/p.php?idx=3 (V5), https://www.allegrohand.com/sub/product/p.php?idx=4 (V5 Plus), https://www.allegrohand.com/sub/product/p.php?idx=2 (V4)
- **Robotiq 3-Finger Adaptive Gripper** — 3-finger underactuated/adaptive gripper with multiple grasp modes (fingertip, wide, encompassing); still listed as a current Robotiq product. Status: commercial. Source: https://robotiq.com/products/adaptive-grippers

## 5-finger anthropomorphic hands — global
- **Shadow Dexterous Hand / DEX-EE series (Shadow Robot Company)** — 20 actuated DOF (24 joints total incl. underactuated movements) plus a 2-DOF wrist, 100+ sensors up to 1 kHz incl. 40 tendon load sensors and tactile fingertips; new DEX-EE / DEX-EE Chiral line developed with Google DeepMind. Status: commercial (research-grade). Source: https://shadowrobot.com/dexterous-hand-series/
- **Schunk SVH** — anthropomorphic 5-finger hand, nine drives, 24 V DC, elastic gripping surfaces, control and power electronics integrated in the wrist; ready for series production, with ROS1 and ROS2 drivers available online per the vendor page (joint count, mass and bus interface are not on that page, so confirm them in the datasheet). Status: commercial. Source: https://schunk.com/us/en/gripping-systems/special-gripper/svh/c/PGR_3161
- **PSYONIC Ability Hand** — 5-finger bionic hand, 490 g, 78 N maximum power-grasp grip force (8 N pinch, 17 N key grasp; 0.2 s finger closing; 45.4 kg axial load capacity) per User Manual v3.0 for hands made after 1/1/2025, while the older v2.23 manual lists 66 N power grasp, so the figure depends on hand generation. Fingertip pressure sensors with haptic feedback, Bluetooth app control; originally a prosthetic, also used as a research/robot end-effector. Status: commercial. Source: https://www.psyonic.io/ability-hand (weight, sensors); specs from https://www.psyonic.io/s/User-Manual-PSYONIC-Ability-Hand-11625-nzjm.pdf (v3.0) and https://www.psyonic.io/s/User-Manual.pdf (v2.23)
- **Wonik Robotics Allegro Hand V6 F** — five-finger, 20-DoF robotic hand from the Allegro line (launched 2026-09-07 per the vendor news page; V5 and V4 remain listed); pressure sensing at fingertips, finger joints and palm (16 pressure sensing channels per the vendor homepage); current, position, stiffness and high-force control modes; RS-485 and Ethernet interfaces with Modbus connectivity (the pages do not mention CAN, which the V5 entry above lists). Status: commercial. Source: https://www.knoxlabs.com/products/wonik-robotics-allegro-hand-v6-f and https://www.allegrohand.com/
- **DH-Robotics ADH-5-13** — five-finger hand with 13 independently controllable active DOF (thumb, index and middle 3 DOF each; ring and little 2 DOF each), fully direct-drive and backdrivable, 735 g, up to 20 kg lifting payload once an object is grasped, fingertip tactile sensing with options for electronic skin, six-axis force/torque and vision-based tactile sensors; global debut at IROS 2026 (Pittsburgh; release dated Sept. 30, 2026). Status: announced (vendor claims; the release gives no availability or pricing). ## Open-source hand designs
- **LEAP Hand** (Shaw, Agarwal, Pathak — CMU) — low-cost anthropomorphic 4-finger hand for robot-learning research; assembles in 4 hours for $2,000 from off-the-shelf parts, reported to outperform Allegro Hand in the paper's experiments at 1/8th the cost. Status: research (open-source hardware + code). Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06440
- **LEAP Hand V2 Advanced** (Shaw, Pathak — CMU) — hybrid rigid-soft anthropomorphic hand with a 3D-printed soft exterior over a 3D-printed internal bone structure; the project page lists 21 DOF with 17 powered motors (the API README calls it 17-DOF), plus two independently powered palm articulations that let it grasp tools tightly. Listed at $3,000 and "can be built within a day" from the published instructions. Not the same as the standard LEAP Hand V2 (a separate 8-DOF, roughly $200 model per the V2 Adv API README). API and IK code: https://github.com/leap-hand/LEAP_Hand_V2_Adv_API (the original LEAP_Hand_API and LEAP_Hand_Sim repos are labelled V1). Status: research (open-source hardware + code). Source: https://v2-adv.leaphand.com/
- **ORCA Hand (ETH Zurich Soft Robotics Lab)** — open-source, 17-DOF tendon-driven anthropomorphic hand with integrated tactile sensors, opposable thumb, actuated wrist; assembles in under 8 hours, material cost below 2,000 CHF, 3D-printable structural parts, joints designed to pop safely under overload instead of breaking. Status: research (open-source hardware). Source: https://srl.ethz.ch/orcahand.html
- **Yale OpenHand Project** — family of underactuated, tendon-driven, 3D-printable research hands (Model T, T42, GR2 gripper etc.) that adaptively conform to objects without explicit sensing/feedback. Status: research (open-source hardware). Source: https://www.eng.yale.edu/grablab/openhand/
- **DexHand.org** — fully open-source humanoid robot hand platform (mechanical CAD/STL, Bluetooth LE firmware, hand-tracking demos, ROS2 packages) released by Rob Knight / The Robot Studio as a low-cost R&D platform for humanoid-hand grasping/manipulation research. Status: maintained (open-source). Source: https://www.dexhand.org/

## Tactile sensors
- **GelSight (Nano / Digit 360, with Meta AI)** — vision-based optical tactile fingertip sensor line; GelSight Nano targets a sub-15 mm-thick fingertip rated for 50,000+ grasps at 50 fps 3D shape/force output, and GelSight received a 2026 US Air Force Phase II SBIR to develop a compact, rugged "digital fingertip" for robotic grasping. Status: commercial + active R&D. Source: https://www.gelsight.com/gelsight-air-force-sbir-robotic-tactile-sensors/
- **Meta DIGIT** — low-cost, compact, high-resolution vision-based tactile sensor for in-hand manipulation, open-sourced by Meta AI (design + code); Digit 360 is the newer, higher-fidelity 18+ sensing-modality successor; Meta published its code and designs in facebookresearch/digit360 and facebookresearch/digit-plexus, and Meta's announcement said GelSight Inc. would manufacture and distribute it (current availability not verified). The original DIGIT repos (facebookresearch/digit-design, last push 2025-03-09; facebookresearch/digit-interface, last push 2021-09-22) and the tacto simulator (last push 2022-02-17) are archived on GitHub, so treat the original DIGIT as buildable from its published manufacturing files but not actively maintained. Status: Digit 360 open-source (facebookresearch/digit360 last push 2025-07-22, digit-plexus 2025-07-02, as of 2026-10-02); original DIGIT software archived. Source: https://ai.meta.com/blog/teaching-robots-to-perceive-understand-and-interact-through-touch/ ; https://ai.meta.com/blog/fair-robotics-open-source/ ; https://github.com/facebookresearch/digit360

## In-hand manipulation & learned dexterity research (arXiv)
- **HANDFUL** — resource-aware, sequential grasp-conditioned dexterous manipulation; models finger usage as a limited resource for multi-skill sequences, validated on a real LEAP Hand. Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.25126
- **SeqMultiGrasp** — sequential multi-object grasping with a 4-fingered Allegro Hand, combining physics-simulator-validated grasp synthesis with a point-cloud-conditioned diffusion model for real-world deployment (65.8% sim / 56.7% real success over many object pairs). Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.09078
- **DexUMI** — uses a human hand (via wearable exoskeleton + video inpainting) as a universal manipulation-data-collection interface, transferring skills to different robot hands with an 86% average real-world task success rate across two hardware platforms. Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.21864
- **FBI (Flow Before Imitation)** — visuotactile imitation-learning framework for dexterous in-hand manipulation that dynamically fuses tactile flow with vision via a dynamics-aware latent model and a one-step diffusion policy. Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.14441
- **AnyDexGrasp** — general dexterous grasping framework achieving human-level learning efficiency (hundreds, not millions, of grasp attempts per hand); reports 75–98% real-world grasp success across three different robotic hands in cluttered scenes. Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.16420

## Teleop/retargeting tools
- **AnyTeleop** — general vision-based dexterous robot arm-hand teleoperation system (RSS 2023) that captures human hand motion from RGB and retargets in real time across different robot embodiments; source of the widely used `dex-retargeting` library below. Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.04577
- **DexPilot** — low-cost, depth-camera-based teleoperation enabling control of a full 23 degree-of-actuation hand-arm system by observing a bare human hand, without gloves/markers. Status: research. Source: https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/DexPilot:-Vision-Based-Teleoperation-of-Dexterous-Handa-Wyk/d413f75610ad07557f54e8145289b22cfa988e5b
- **Open-TeleVision** — immersive teleoperation system (CoRL 2024) giving the operator stereoscopic visual feedback of the robot's surroundings while mirroring arm/hand motion onto the robot; open-sourced, validated on two humanoid robots across four long-horizon manipulation tasks for imitation-learning data collection. Status: research (open-source; last commit on main 2024-09-27, no releases, as of 2026-10-02). Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.01512
- **dex-retargeting** — MIT-licensed open-source library of retargeting optimizers that map human hand poses to diverse robot hand morphologies, originating from the AnyTeleop project. Status: open-source, slow-moving (last PyPI release 0.5.0 on 2025-04-05, no versioned GitHub releases (only a rolling nightly build), last commit on main 2025-04-05; open issues #89 and #88 from Mar 2026 report mediapipe 0.10.3x breaking the hand detector, so pin the mediapipe version). Source: https://github.com/dexsuite/dex-retargeting
- **Analyzing Key Objectives in Human-to-Robot Retargeting for Dexterous Manipulation** (2025) — ablates vector objectives on Leap and Shadow hands with Vision Pro input: the thumb-to-finger pinch term is essential (with distance clamping near contact), DIP-joint-to-fingertip vectors fix fingertip orientation, thumb-tip position beats wrist position, joint-velocity regularization plus an EMA (alpha 0.3 at 20 Hz) removes acceleration spikes. Fingertip-only contacts. Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09384
- **GeoRT (Geometric Retargeting)** (2025) — neural keypoint-to-joint retargeting trained unsupervised per hand pair, ~1 kHz, objectives for motion fidelity, C-space coverage, flatness, pinch correspondence and self-collision. Trades test-time optimization for a per-hand training step. Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07541
- **TopoRetarget** (2026) — interaction-preserving retargeting for contact-rich demos: builds an interaction mesh from hand keypoints and object surface samples so contacts on fingertips, intermediate phalanges, finger sides and palm transfer; needs the object mesh and pose trajectory, so not for object-free teleop. ~4.7 ms/frame; validated on Wuji Hand hardware from MediaPipe 21 keypoints. Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16272
- **Kinematic Motion Retargeting for Contact-Rich Anthropomorphic Manipulations** (Lakshmipathy, Hodgins, Pollard, 2024; full text read 2026-09-09) — offline pipeline (2–22 h per motion on one CPU, finite-difference gradients) that transfers dense per-frame contact areas from GRAB-style hand-object data to five hands (incl. Allegro) via an atlas of log-map charts along artist-annotated finger axial curves, with per-finger length (λ_A) and girth (λ_S) scaling; objective = virtual-marker L2 + contact point L2 + contact-normal inversion + table SDF + rest-pose prior (λ_J 50 vs 1), MMA in NLopt, frames solved independently then acceleration-filtered and B-spline fitted. Ablations: contact term essential (marker-only fails to conform to objects); fingertip keypoint tracking and DexPilot keyvectors "struggle to make contact" when geometries diverge. Needs object mesh + contacts; not for live teleop, but the axial-curve contact parameterization and independent-per-frame solving transfer. Status: research. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04820
- Practical note (2026-09-09, HX5-D20 glove teleop): with fingertip-only pinch vectors the robot thumb goes to a fingertip when the human thumb touches a finger's middle phalanx. Object-free fix that worked: per-finger contact candidates (tip, DIP, PIP landmark ↔ robot tip, DIP joint, PIP joint), per-candidate on/off distance thresholds (phalanx landmarks are close to the thumb even in an open hand), and blending of optimizer solutions across candidates.
